What to expect on the first call

A 10–20 minute honest conversation.

Jorge will ask what you're trying to accomplish — buy, sell, relocate, get a valuation, or explore. He'll answer whatever questions you have and tell you what a realistic timeline and price range look like for your specific situation.

There's no listing agreement to sign, no pressure to make an offer, no commitment beyond the call. If it makes sense to keep talking, you'll both know. If not, you'll still walk away with useful information about your market.

Response-time guarantee

A reply within 24 hours — usually much faster.

During business hours, texts and calls get returned within a few hours. Emails sent after 9pm get answered the next morning. If you send a message overnight or on a holiday, you'll hear back within 24 hours — that's the guarantee.

In an active buying or selling situation, expect faster. Deals move on nights and weekends in NJ, and Jorge does too.

Frequent questions

Before you reach out.

Do I need to be committed to selling or buying?

No. A lot of the calls Jorge takes are from people who are 6–18 months out — just gathering information. That's fine. Getting the lay of the land early usually leads to a better outcome than waiting until you're rushed.

What if I'm just exploring — testing the water?

That's a normal call. Jorge would rather have an early no-pressure conversation than get a rushed lead six months from now. You can ask about your neighborhood, your current home value, a specific listing you saw on Zillow, or how the process works — no follow-up unless you want one.

Do you charge for a consultation or a valuation?

No. First conversations, market analyses, and home valuations are free. If you decide to work with Jorge on a purchase or a sale, the standard NJ commission structure applies and is fully disclosed before anything is signed.
