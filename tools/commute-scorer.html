Before deciding

Four checks the worksheet cannot do for you

01

Check a dated itinerary

Use the actual origin, destination, day, and departure window. Weekday, weekend, and special-service patterns may differ.

02

Count every leg

Include the trip to the first stop, waiting, transfers, the scheduled ride, the final leg, and a buffer that reflects your needs.

03

Verify current costs

Check current fares, tolls, parking terms, and connecting-service costs. Enter daily round-trip amounts on the same basis for every option.

04

Test the trip

When practical, make the route during the travel window that matters to you and note what the published itinerary did not capture.

Keep the comparison personal and property-specific

Use your own work location, schedule, mobility needs, budget, and property criteria. The calculation uses only the trip components you enter; it does not assess people, schools, reported crime, or neighborhood character.

Research

Official source notebook

Open the operating agency's current information before entering a figure. Each source has a specific job and a limit.

NJ TRANSIT

Trip Planner and Service Near a Location

Use: Check a current itinerary for the specific origin, destination, date, and departure time.

Limit: A planned itinerary can change. Recheck it near the travel date and review current service notices.

NJ TRANSIT

Schedules and Fares

Use: Verify current rail, bus, or light-rail schedules and fare information for the route being compared.

Limit: This worksheet does not import live schedules or fares; enter only figures you have checked at the source.

NJ TRANSIT

My Transit Alerts

Use: Review the agency's options for route, schedule-change, delay, and station advisories.

Limit: An alert service helps with current notices; it cannot predict future reliability for a property search.

Port Authority of New York and New Jersey

PATH schedules, maps, fares, and station updates

Use: Check current PATH information when a comparison includes Newark, Harrison, Jersey City, or Hoboken service.

Limit: PATH and NJ TRANSIT are separate systems; verify each leg with the agency operating it.
