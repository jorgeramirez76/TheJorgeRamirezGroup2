## 6. One cannibalization fix that needs your judgment (don't rush it)

Your NYC→NJ moving content is split across two English pages that both rank
poorly (positions 70–90), splitting your authority:
- `/nyc-to-nj-relocation` (3,600 words, Google's preferred — more impressions)
- `/blog/moving-from-nyc-to-nj-guide` (5,300 words, buried, tied to the Spanish
  version via hreflang)

**Recommended fix (careful, manual):** merge the blog guide's extra content into
`/nyc-to-nj-relocation`, then 301-redirect the blog guide to it, and repoint the
Spanish page's hreflang to `/nyc-to-nj-relocation`. Doing this blind risks your
international (hreflang) signals, so it's flagged here rather than auto-applied.
~940 impressions/month of "moving to NJ" searches are at stake — worth doing
right. Ask Claude to do this as a focused task when you're ready.
