Chatham Township, New Jersey

A land-and-address guide for Township maps, open-space documents, recreation sites, school confirmation, and a rail plan that correctly locates the Chatham station.

Updated August 25, 2026 · No Borough facts are assigned to the Township

Read the address precisely

Township is not shorthand for Borough

Chatham Township is a separate municipality from Chatham Borough. Its municipal building, assessment records, land-use maps, parks, and open-space materials are Township resources. The shared Chatham name—and the shared school district—does not merge the governments.

The separate Chatham Borough guide covers the Borough and Front Street station setting. The broader Chatham page should not be used to decide which municipal rules govern a particular Township parcel.

Municipal identity

Start at 58 Meyersville Road

The Township of Chatham website identifies its municipal building at 58 Meyersville Road and directs residents to Township departments. Use the Township—not Borough—assessor and map library when researching a Chatham Township property.

A multi-layer map check

The Township's official maps page links zoning and tax maps, a watershed map, Morris County GIS, NJDEP GIS, and FEMA resources. Compare layers, then ask the issuing agency what the current record means for the parcel.

Assessment questions

Direct current value-record and ownership questions to the Chatham Township Tax Assessor. Use the record as one input alongside the deed, title material, survey, and inspection.

Township geography

U.S. Census QuickFacts identifies Chatham Township, Morris County, as a distinct geography. Do not combine this dataset with the separate Chatham Borough geography.

Use the legal description

Postal wording, a portal label, or a nearby landmark can be imprecise. Confirm municipality, block and lot, easements, and property boundaries through transaction documents and official records.

Parks and civic resources

Township facilities and open-space documents

The Township parks and recreation page identifies Colony Pool and local sites including Nash Park, Shunpike Field, Esternay Field, and Mountainview Field. Current operations, registration, and access rules belong with the Township department.

Open-space evidence

The Open Space Committee document library publishes plans, inventories, and trail material. Use those records to frame questions about a nearby mapped area without promising preservation or future use.

Visit from the property

Walk or drive the lawful route to any facility important to the decision. Check entrances, grade, crossings, operating rules, and whether a map line corresponds to public access.

Transportation research

Build the trip from a Township doorstep

NJ TRANSIT places Chatham Station on Front Street. Chatham Station is in Chatham Borough, and the station page identifies Chatham Borough as the municipal parking contact. Township residents should not assume Borough parking eligibility or a uniform station route.

Route before schedule

Map the exact property to the chosen station, bus stop, or destination. Include the first and last segment, the desired departure window, transfers, and service alerts.

Parking is its own decision

Contact the listed operator about current permits, residency conditions, payment, and restrictions. Treat a listing's parking statement as a lead to verify, not a transportation guarantee.

Public-school research

One shared district, one exact address

Chatham Township and Chatham Borough share the School District of the Chathams. The shared district does not erase municipal identity. Ask the district to confirm registration and the current school assignment tied to the Township property.

Operational answer

Use district contacts for enrollment, attendance boundaries, calendars, transportation policies, and documents that can change. Record the date and source of any address-specific answer.

State data

The New Jersey School Performance Reports offer school-level state data and methodology. They should inform research without turning educational data into a neighborhood recommendation.

Property due diligence

Connect the survey to the map layers

Land-use file

  • Match the survey and legal description to the Township tax map.
  • Check the zoning district before assuming an addition, accessory use, fence, or other project is permitted.
  • Ask about permits, approvals, easements, and any open municipal matters relevant to the transaction.
  • Confirm utility responsibility and service details for the exact address with the applicable providers.

Water-context file

  • Compare Township watershed and GIS resources with the parcel and observed drainage.
  • Use the NJDEP Flood Indicator Tool only as an initial screen.
  • Review disclosures, insurance questions, drainage features, and specialist findings as the property warrants.
  • Remember that no indicator on a screening map is not proof of no risk.

Verify current tax data, zoning rules, flood conditions, transit schedules, and school assignment directly with the responsible public agency before relying on them.

Decision tools

Township-specific transaction questions

Buyer decision checklist

  • Confirm Chatham Township on the deed and assessor record.
  • Overlay the survey with zoning, watershed, tax, and environmental mapping.
  • Test the route to Chatham Station or another chosen transit point from the driveway.
  • Ask the district for written address-based school confirmation.
  • Inspect site drainage and reconcile disclosures with professional findings.
  • Check actual access and rules for nearby Township recreation facilities.

Seller decision checklist

  • Use Township records and avoid Borough department links in property materials.
  • Gather the survey, permits, approvals, easements, repair records, and utility documents.
  • Describe open-space adjacency carefully without predicting future land status.
  • State transit access as a route to a Borough station, not a station inside the Township.
  • Complete current property and flood disclosures with appropriate professional advice.

Chatham Township questions

Answers that preserve municipal boundaries

Is Chatham Township governed by Chatham Borough?

No. Chatham Township and Chatham Borough are separate municipalities. Chatham Township maintains its own government, assessor, maps, and land-use resources.

Does Chatham Township contain Chatham Station?

Chatham Station is in Chatham Borough. Township residents should map the route from the exact property and confirm current parking eligibility and rail service with NJ TRANSIT and the Borough.

Where can I check a Chatham Township parcel's zoning and environmental context?

Start with the Township maps page for zoning, tax, watershed, county GIS, state GIS, and FEMA links, then ask the responsible agency for parcel-specific confirmation.

Which source confirms public-school assignment in Chatham Township?

Contact the School District of the Chathams with the property address. Use state School Performance Reports for state-published data, not for assignment confirmation.
