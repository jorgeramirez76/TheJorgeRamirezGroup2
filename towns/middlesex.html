Town-specific property review

Keep Borough assessment and permit questions in their proper records

Use the Borough finance and assessment resource to identify the current record and responsible contact for the parcel. Reconcile it with the deed, survey, title material, and current tax information; an assessment field is not a condition report or value opinion.

Permit and land-use trail

Use the official Borough directory to locate the office that maintains zoning, construction, code, certificate, or public-record information relevant to the observed use and planned work. Ask the office to apply the current rule to the exact address.

Comparable geography

Assemble the Middlesex Borough seller file before pricing

A useful pricing review combines current comparable evidence with what can be verified about the subject property. It does not convert a county statistic or automated estimate into a promised sale price.

  • Confirm the Middlesex Borough address, block and lot, current assessment, and tax record.
