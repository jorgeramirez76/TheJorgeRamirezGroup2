Guttenberg · Hudson County, New Jersey

A Guttenberg property research guide

For a geographically compact town, an address can still raise layered questions about the unit, building, parcel, municipal approvals, bus stop, school grade, and shared property obligations. These official links help keep each answer tied to the correct institution.

Confirm the Town of Guttenberg on every record. Guttenberg is its own Hudson County municipality. Do not let a nearby place name, postal result, transit stop, or building marketing label redirect municipal due diligence. The Town’s official website is the starting point for departments, public notices, agendas, meetings, and current contacts.

Three municipal files

Land use, construction, and assessment answer different questions

Before treating an observed layout or use as established, connect it to the board record, construction file, and parcel data that apply. These sources complement rather than replace a title search, survey where relevant, physical inspection, attorney review, and association documents.

Joint Planning and Zoning Board

The official board page links the master plan, zoning map, applications, notices, and land-use records. Check current zoning, use standards, resolutions, conditions, variances, and pending matters for the subject property and any nearby proposal relevant to your decision.

Building Department

The Building Department provides construction, inspection, zoning-map, certificate, and short-term-rental resources. Ask about the unit and common building areas when work or occupancy may involve both. A renovated appearance does not demonstrate that permits were required, closed, or transferable.

Tax Assessor

The Tax Assessor page covers assessment records, ownership and lot data, exemptions, and appeals. Confirm the parcel or unit identifier, current assessment record, and tax information with the responsible office. Do not infer future tax treatment from a neighboring unit or prior owner.

The U.S. Census Bureau’s Guttenberg profile offers government-published housing, population, and geography context. Review each metric’s vintage and definition; municipality-level information should not be used to characterize a particular building, household, or block.

Route-specific travel check

Read Route 188 as a timetable, not a commute promise

Guttenberg to the bus-station network

The current NJ TRANSIT Route 188 timetable identifies Guttenberg stops and service toward the George Washington Bridge Bus Station. Select the right direction and read the service-day headings, timepoints, footnotes, and trip pattern. A listed town does not mean every trip stops at every location.

Inspect the first and last segment

Walk from the building entrance to the intended stop and repeat the route in the return direction. Evaluate grade, crossings, sidewalks, pickup side, shelter, lighting, accessibility needs, and current alerts. Transfers and the onward trip should be checked with the operating agencies involved.

Vehicle and delivery questions

For a condo or multi-unit building, identify the documentary basis for parking or garage access. Confirm whether a space is deeded, assigned, licensed, or leased and whether guest, loading, move, and delivery rules apply. Municipal street rules and private building rules are separate checks.

Verify changes close to travel

Transit schedules and alerts can change. Recheck the Route 188 document and NJ TRANSIT notices shortly before using the service. For a purchase decision, test the trip under the conditions that match the rider’s expected schedule rather than relying only on a map.

School and town resources

Keep grade-level and recreation facts current

The district and Recreation Department publish different types of public information. Use each source for its stated purpose, confirm dates and rules, and avoid using either to make assumptions about the people who may live at an address.

Anna L. Klein School and district contacts

The Guttenberg School District’s official site identifies Anna L. Klein School as the district’s preschool-through-eighth-grade school and provides registration information. Because arrangements beyond that span can change, contact the district to verify current school assignment, registration, transportation, and the responsible district for every grade. The NJDOE School Performance Reports provide state-published fields with their definitions and reporting years.

Waterfront Park and other civic sites

The Town Recreation Department is the official source for programs and municipal references including Waterfront Park at 7100 River Road, Monument Park near Boulevard East and 71st Street, other municipal park space, and the Drasheff Community Center at 301 69th Street. Confirm current access, activities, registration, reservations, and rules.

Condo and building review

Look above, below, inside, and beyond the unit

A unit inspection may not cover every common system or legal obligation. Combine the physical review with the association package, municipal file, master insurance, title work, lender requirements, and counsel’s document review.

Common systems and boundaries

Clarify responsibility for windows, façade, roof, balconies, plumbing lines, heating or cooling equipment, elevators, parking, storage, and utilities. Compare what the governing documents say with the inspection scope and building records. Ask who pays a deductible or repair when more than one unit is affected.

Association finances and rules

Review the budget, reserves, insurance, meeting minutes, litigation, fees, pending or approved assessments, delinquency information where provided, leasing provisions, pet rules, move procedures, and renovation approvals. Check document dates and obtain updated information before closing.

Flood, water, and coverage

Start with NJDEP’s flood-disclosure resources and property screening, then ask about the building site, lower levels, equipment, drainage, water history, flood coverage, and unit-owner insurance. The screening tool is not a calculation of actual risk, and the absence of an indicator is not a guarantee.

Decision controls

Guttenberg buyer and seller verification lists

If you are buying

  • Confirm Guttenberg jurisdiction, address, parcel or unit identifier, deed, title description, and survey if applicable.
  • Ask the district to verify the current school assignment and registration process for the relevant grade.
  • Investigate zoning, use, board approvals, permits, certificates, and planned alterations with the proper Town offices.
  • Use the current Route 188 timetable and test the actual stop, direction, transfer, and walking route.
  • Review association governance, finances, assessments, insurance, parking rights, rules, minutes, and unit records.
  • Confirm taxes and complete inspection, title, flood, environmental, lender, and insurance review by the applicable deadlines.

If you are selling

  • Prepare the deed, assessment and tax information, permits, certificates, renovation records, warranties, and disclosures.
  • Request the current resale package and understand association timing, fees, approvals, move rules, and balances.
  • Describe unit boundaries, parking, storage, views, utilities, and amenities only as the documents support them.
  • Use official school, recreation, and transit links without subjective rankings or guarantees.
  • Review flood and property disclosures with counsel where a response or building history is uncertain.
  • Address discrepancies between the observed layout and municipal or association records before they delay a transaction.

Frequently asked questions

Guttenberg research questions

Where can I verify Guttenberg zoning and permit records?

Use the Town’s Joint Planning and Zoning Board and Building Department resources, then ask the responsible office about the exact parcel, unit, use, and proposed work.

What does the official Guttenberg school source cover?

The district identifies Anna L. Klein School as its preschool-through-eighth-grade school. Contact the district to verify registration, assignment, and current arrangements for every grade level.

How should I check Route 188 from Guttenberg?

Read NJ TRANSIT’s current Route 188 timetable for Guttenberg stops, directions, trip patterns, service days, and the George Washington Bridge Bus Station routing.
