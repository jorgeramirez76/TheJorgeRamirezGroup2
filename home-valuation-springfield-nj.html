Why Online Estimates Get Springfield Wrong

Automated estimates can't walk your block. In Springfield, two homes a quarter-mile apart can differ by six figures based on school catchment, distance to bus express routes + Short Hills/Millburn train access, and what's behind the walls. Algorithms also lag the market — in a town appreciating +4% a year, a 90-day-old comp is already stale. That is why Jorge prepares a local CMA instead of relying on an automated estimate.

What Actually Drives Springfield Home Values in 2026

  • Commute: bus express routes + Short Hills/Millburn train access keeps NYC buyer demand constant — proximity to the station carries a measurable premium.
  • Recent renovations: kitchens, baths, and systems (roof, HVAC) move the appraisal more than square footage in this market.
  • Inventory scarcity: Springfield listings are limited, which is why correctly priced homes draw multiple offers in 30-48 days.
  • Property taxes: buyers underwrite the monthly payment, so your tax bill relative to neighboring blocks affects what they'll pay.
