NJ Counties We Cover — The Jorge Ramirez Group

County-level real estate market guides for the six New Jersey counties Jorge Ramirez serves: Essex, Hudson, Middlesex, Morris, Somerset, and Union.

Jorge's day-to-day coverage spans NJ towns across these six counties — the corridor most people picture when they think of a NJ commute to New York or Newark. Each county has its own personality, price band, and buyer profile. Below is a quick read on each, plus a link into its full town-by-town breakdown.

Essex County

Essex County includes higher-priced suburban housing and dense urban markets. Millburn/Short Hills, Montclair, Maplewood, and South Orange have local school districts and rail service; compare current prices, NJ Transit schedules, taxes, and address-specific school information independently.

Representative towns: Millburn · Short Hills · Montclair · Maplewood · South Orange · Livingston · West Orange · Verona · Glen Ridge

Hudson County

Hudson is the urban-lifestyle end of Jorge's coverage — condos and townhouses over single-family, PATH and ferry over rail. Hoboken and downtown Jersey City compete directly with Manhattan on walkability and nightlife at a meaningful discount. Buyers here are typically buyers comparing transit and employment access, first-time buyers priced out of Brooklyn, and investors looking at rental yields.

Representative towns: Hoboken · Jersey City · Weehawken · Union City · West New York · Bayonne

Middlesex County

Middlesex County includes varied lot sizes, housing types, municipal services, district structures, and Northeast Corridor access. Compare current price-per-square-foot data, transit schedules, taxes, and property condition for the same property type and date range.

Representative towns: Metuchen · Edison · Highland Park · East Brunswick · Woodbridge · South Plainfield · Piscataway

Morris County

Morris is where buyers go for more house, more land, and a slightly quieter feel while staying on the Midtown Direct. Chatham and Madison are the flagship school towns; Morristown offers walkable downtown living with a train station. Further west, Mendham, Chester, and Long Valley get you real acreage. Buyers here are typically families upgrading to their forever-home phase and NYC exiles who want a real yard.

Representative towns: Chatham · Madison · Morristown · Florham Park · Mendham · Chester · Long Valley · Denville

Somerset County

Somerset County includes varied housing types, corporate-campus access, highway connections, rail-served areas, and lower-density municipalities. Compare current prices, taxes, transit, lot size, municipal services, and address-specific NJDOE school information independently.

Representative towns: Bernardsville · Basking Ridge · Bridgewater · Far Hills · Peapack-Gladstone · Warren

Union County

Union County includes Midtown Direct and Raritan Valley Line municipalities with different housing types, station access, taxes, district structures, and municipal services. Compare current property-specific prices and official sources rather than relying on a countywide tier or buyer profile.

Representative towns: Summit · Westfield · Cranford · New Providence · Berkeley Heights · Roselle Park · Kenilworth · Scotch Plains · Fanwood
