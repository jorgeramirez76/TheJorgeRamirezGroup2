NJ Counties We Cover — The Jorge Ramirez Group

County-level real estate market guides for the six New Jersey counties Jorge Ramirez serves: Essex, Hudson, Middlesex, Morris, Somerset, and Union.

Use the same objective criteria across all six counties: current property-specific pricing and taxes, housing type and condition, official transit schedules, municipal services, and address-specific school information from the district or NJ Department of Education. Each county link opens its community directory; it is not a neighborhood ranking or recommendation.

Essex County

Essex County includes municipalities with different housing types, property-tax obligations, district structures, and transit options. Compare homes of a similar type and condition, then verify NJ Transit service and address-specific school information with the relevant official source.

Communities covered in the directory: Millburn · Short Hills · Montclair · Maplewood · South Orange · Livingston · West Orange · Verona · Glen Ridge

Hudson County

Hudson County has varied property types and ownership structures, including condominiums, cooperative units, townhouses, multifamily buildings, and single-family homes. Transit options may include PATH, light rail, ferries, or buses depending on the address; confirm schedules, fares, property documents, taxes, and current housing costs directly.

Communities covered in the directory: Hoboken · Jersey City · Weehawken · Union City · West New York · Bayonne

Middlesex County

Middlesex County includes varied lot sizes, housing types, municipal services, district structures, and Northeast Corridor access. Compare current price-per-square-foot data, transit schedules, taxes, and property condition for the same property type and date range.

Communities covered in the directory: Metuchen · Edison · Highland Park · East Brunswick · Woodbridge · South Plainfield · Piscataway

Morris County

Morris County includes municipalities with varied housing types, lot sizes, property conditions, district structures, and rail, bus, or highway access. Compare current property-specific costs and taxes, and use official transit and school sources for the address and travel time that matter to you.

Communities covered in the directory: Chatham · Madison · Morristown · Florham Park · Mendham · Chester · Long Valley · Denville

Somerset County

Somerset County includes varied housing types, lot sizes, highway connections, rail-served areas, municipal services, and district structures. Compare current property-specific prices, taxes, transit, condition, and address-specific NJDOE or district information independently.

Communities covered in the directory: Bernardsville · Basking Ridge · Bridgewater · Far Hills · Peapack-Gladstone · Warren

Union County

Union County includes Midtown Direct and Raritan Valley Line municipalities with different housing types, station access, taxes, district structures, and municipal services. Compare current property-specific prices, condition, taxes, and official schedules rather than relying on a countywide tier.

Communities covered in the directory: Summit · Westfield · Cranford · New Providence · Berkeley Heights · Roselle Park · Kenilworth · Scotch Plains · Fanwood
