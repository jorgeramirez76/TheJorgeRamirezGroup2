Start with the geography, then narrow to the address

A Somerset County statistic is regional context. It does not describe every municipality, neighborhood label, property type, or individual address.

01

County

Use a county report for a dated regional reference. Keep the period and property category attached to any figure you record.

02

Municipality or locality

Confirm the legal municipality. A postal or neighborhood name may sit inside a differently named municipality with different records.

03

Property

Verify the parcel, assessment, permits, condition, disclosures, and genuinely comparable sales for the address itself.

Five source checks that answer different questions

Market reports

Select the county, reporting period, and available property category. Do not reuse county figures as town-level facts.

Property-tax records

Read the state table labels and the current municipal record. An average is not a parcel's bill or a valuation.

District reports

Search the state report system, then confirm the current district and attendance assignment for the specific address.

Transit

Test the actual origin, destination, departure time, transfers, and current service notices in NJ TRANSIT's tools.

Place identity

A buyer research sequence

  1. Confirm the legal municipality and the property's exact address.
  2. Check live transit options for the trips that matter to you.
  3. Review current district reports and verify the address assignment directly.
  4. Read the current tax record, assessment history, and available permit records.
  5. Compare recent, property-specific sales and active alternatives with the same property type and relevant features.

A seller pricing sequence

  1. Document the property's condition, updates, constraints, and timing priorities.
  2. Separate county headlines from municipality- and property-specific evidence.
  3. Compare recent closed sales, pending evidence when available, and current competing listings.
  4. Review presentation, disclosure, access, and launch decisions before selecting a list-price strategy.
  5. Recheck the evidence when market conditions or the competing inventory changes.

Source notebook

Each source has a defined job and a limit. Open the original source, record its date and geography, and avoid extending it beyond what it actually reports.

Somerset County

Somerset County Official Directory of County and Municipal Offices

Use: Use the county directory to locate the legal municipality and responsible public office.

Limit: A directory identifies government resources; it does not describe a property's market value or condition.

New Jersey Realtors / ShowingTime Plus

Public state and county market reports

Use: Select a county, reporting period, and available property category at the source.

Limit: County reports do not establish a municipality-level result or a value for a particular property.

New Jersey Department of the Treasury, Division of Taxation

Property Tax Statistics

Use: Review the state's annual property-tax tables and their labels.

Limit: Averages, assessments, and equalization data are not a listing price, appraisal, or future sale result.

New Jersey Department of Education

School Performance Reports

Use: Search current state-published reports by school, district, county, or ZIP code.

Limit: Confirm the district and attendance assignment for the specific address; a county page cannot make that determination.

NJ TRANSIT

Trip Planner and Service Near a Location

Use: Check the current route, transfer, stop, schedule, and service notices for an actual trip.

Limit: Travel time varies by origin, destination, departure, transfers, and service conditions.

State of New Jersey

Locality Search

Use: Confirm whether a place name is a legal municipality or a locality within another municipality.

Limit: Postal and neighborhood names do not always match legal municipal boundaries.
