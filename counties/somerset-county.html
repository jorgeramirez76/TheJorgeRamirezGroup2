Somerset County Real Estate Market Overview

Somerset County consistently ranks among the most desirable counties in New Jersey for homebuyers, driven by its proximity to New York City, strong public school systems, and mix of suburban character with urban convenience.

The county spans a wide range of price points. On the premium end, Bernardsville, Bedminster, and Far Hills homes routinely sell above $1.5 million — fueled by executive-corridor demand and horse-country cachet. Basking Ridge, with its acclaimed Ridge High School and Gladstone Branch access, sees strong demand from corporate relocations. Bridgewater and Hillsborough are among the fastest-moving family markets in the county.

For buyers seeking value, towns like Bound Brook, Manville, and Raritan have seen significant growth as buyers get priced out of Bernards Township and Bridgewater. These communities offer NJ Transit Raritan Valley Line access, improving downtowns, and home prices that are a fraction of their premium neighbors. Warren Township and Watchung attract families with excellent schools and larger lot sizes, while Somerville offers a walkable downtown revival with easy access to I-287 and Route 22.

Somerset County's central location — bordered by Morris County to the north, Middlesex to the east, Hunterdon to the west, and Union to the northeast — gives residents easy access to Newark Liberty International Airport, major highways (I-78, I-287, Route 202/206), and both the Jersey Shore and New York City.

Frequently Asked Questions About Somerset County Real Estate

What is the average home price in Somerset County NJ?

Somerset County home prices vary significantly by town. Premium executive communities like Bernardsville, Bedminster, and Far Hills typically see median prices from $1M to over $2 million, while towns like Bound Brook, Manville, and Raritan offer more affordable options in the $400,000 to $600,000 range. The county's price diversity is one of its strengths — there is something for first-time buyers, growing families, and luxury seekers. Which Somerset County towns are best for the NYC commute?

Somerset County commuters have two main NJ Transit options. The Gladstone Branch serves Peapack-Gladstone, Far Hills, Bernardsville, and Basking Ridge (Bernards Township) with a transfer at Summit for Midtown Direct service to Penn Station. The Raritan Valley Line runs through Bound Brook, Somerville, Raritan, and Bridgewater with a transfer at Newark Penn. Total commute times to NYC Penn typically range from 60 to 90 minutes depending on town and time of day.

How long does it take to sell a house in Somerset County NJ?

In competitive Somerset County towns like Bridgewater, Basking Ridge, and Bernardsville, well-priced homes can sell in under 30 days, often with multiple offers. Less competitive markets or overpriced listings may take 60 to 90 days. The key is pricing correctly on day one and getting maximum exposure to qualified buyers.
