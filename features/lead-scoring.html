How It Works

Every lead that enters your pipeline — from IDX, Zillow, Facebook Lead Ads, Google, your website, or a manual add — gets scored automatically on a 0-100 scale, then bucketed A, B, C, or D.

  1. Signal capture. The system watches property views, saved searches, calculator usage, reply speed, and how many times a lead opens your emails.
  2. Behavioral scoring. A lead who views the same house three times in 48 hours scores higher than one who signed up six weeks ago and went quiet.
  3. Priority queue. Your dashboard opens to A-tier leads first — the buyers and sellers most likely to transact in the next 30-60 days.
  4. Auto-nurture on the rest. B, C, and D leads drop into drip sequences so nothing is dropped, but you're not wasting live-dial minutes on them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI decide a lead is "hot"?

The score combines behavioral signals (property views, revisits, reply speed) with static signals (source, budget range, time-on-list). Leads who show buying-window behavior — repeated views of the same property, mortgage calculator usage, direct-question replies — score above 75.

Does it work with Zillow, Realtor.com, and Facebook leads?

Yes. AI Sales Pipeline ingests leads from Zillow Premier Agent, Realtor.com, Facebook Lead Ads, Google Ads, IDX sites, and any source with an email parser or webhook. All of them get scored the same way.

Can I override the score manually?

Yes. You can pin a lead to A-tier if you know something the system doesn't (referral from a past client, off-market conversation, etc.). Manual pins are respected but re-checked weekly.

What happens to low-scoring leads?

They stay in your pipeline on automated nurture — email drips, market updates, occasional check-ins from the AI Brain. When a D-tier lead starts showing A-tier behavior (new property views, replies), they get auto-promoted and land on your call list.
