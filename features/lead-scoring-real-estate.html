Built for Real Estate Agents

Real estate has a specific problem that generic CRM lead scoring doesn't solve: most of your inbound leads are 3-9 months away from a transaction, but a few are 30 days out — and you don't know which is which. A buyer who saved five houses in one town this week is closer to writing an offer than a seller who filled out a "what's my home worth" form six months ago. Lead Scoring inside AI Sales Pipeline was built for that exact triage problem, tuned on real estate signals: property views, saved searches, mortgage-calculator usage, and reply speed to your outreach.

How It Works for Agents

  1. Buyer inquiries score by intent. A lead who clicks the same listing three times, saves two more like it, and opens your reply within an hour scores A-tier. A lead who submitted an IDX form and went dark scores C.
  2. Seller inquiries score by urgency. Home-valuation form fills get scored on how deep they explored (comps page, calculator, address entered), plus how quickly they engage with your follow-up.
  3. Past clients get a separate track. Sphere-of-influence contacts don't need scoring — they need cadence. The system flags them for check-ins, not for lead-triage calls.
  4. Your morning list is 10 names, not 400. Open the CRM at 8am and the top of the queue is the buyers and sellers who moved on your criteria overnight.

Real Estate Signals We Score

  • Property page views and revisits (same listing viewed 3+ times = high intent)
  • Saved-search activity and IDX search patterns
  • Mortgage calculator and affordability-tool usage
  • Home-valuation form depth (partial vs. full submission)
  • Reply speed and channel (SMS reply in under an hour scores highest)
  • Email open and click behavior on listing alerts
  • Direct questions in messages ("Is this still available?" "Can we see it Saturday?")
  • Source quality — referrals and organic score above cold portal leads

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from Zillow's Best of Zillow badge?

Zillow scores agents. AI Sales Pipeline scores your leads. Every buyer or seller in your CRM gets a real-time 0-100 score based on their behavior across your website, IDX, and email, so you know who to call first — regardless of which portal they came from.

Do I have to change my current lead sources?

No. Keep Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook Lead Ads, Google, and your IDX website exactly as they are. AI Sales Pipeline ingests all of them and applies scoring on top. You just get a smarter view of the same leads.

Does it work for listing appointments too?

Yes. Seller-side leads (home valuation form fills, "sell my home" inquiries) are scored on urgency signals: how thoroughly they explored value tools, address specificity, timeline questions, and reply engagement. High-score sellers surface for a same-day call, not a same-week email drip.

Can I customize what counts as a hot lead in my market?

Yes. Score weights are adjustable — for example, if you work a rural market where cash buyers dominate, you can turn up the weight on price-range signals. Most agents start with the default real estate model and tune from there.
