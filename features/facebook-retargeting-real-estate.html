Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from boosting a Facebook post?

Boosting shows your post to broad, mostly cold audiences. Retargeting shows purpose-built ads only to people who already visited your website or interacted with your listings. Same platform, different intent — retargeting converts warm traffic that boosting misses entirely.

Can I use this alongside my Zillow or Realtor.com ads?

Yes. Retargeting layers on top of any lead-gen source. Leads that come from Zillow, Realtor.com, or Google that visit your IDX site get added to warm audiences automatically. You get a second, cheaper touch on leads you already paid for.

Is this fair-housing compliant for real estate ads?

Yes. All campaigns run under Facebook's Special Ad Category for Housing, which limits demographic targeting per HUD requirements. Retargeting to your own warm audiences (based on behavior on your website) is fully compliant because it's not targeting protected demographic classes.

What if I don't have a lot of website traffic yet?

Retargeting works even with small audiences (1,000+ visitors/month). Below that, the system uses lookalike audiences — Facebook finds people who behave like your past buyers or sellers — to bootstrap volume until your organic traffic grows.
