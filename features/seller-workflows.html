Seller Workflows are the outreach engine that runs the persistent, professional follow-up your competition isn't willing to do — for home-valuation leads, expired listings, FSBOs, downsizers, and past clients — so more of the seller opportunities in your market turn into signed listing agreements with your name on them.

How It Works

1. Home-valuation follow-up. When someone requests a home value on your site, the workflow sends an instant valuation, a personal video from you, and a "when's the right time to talk" text — then follows up on day 3, day 7, day 14, and monthly if they're not ready yet. You don't have to remember any of it.

2. Expired-listing outreach. The workflow ingests expireds from your MLS daily and runs a multi-touch cadence (letter, text, email, personal video, phone-task) with the messaging tuned to why the last agent's listing didn't sell.

3. FSBO nurture. New For-Sale-By-Owners get a "no pressure — here's what I'd do differently if it doesn't sell" sequence that stays in touch weekly for 60-90 days, positioning you as the agent they call when they're ready to give it up.

4. Past-client and downsizer touch. Anniversary check-ins, market updates, and annual home-value reports keep you top-of-mind with the database that produces referrals and repeat listings — automatically, forever, without you having to write it every year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the messaging different from typical seller drip campaigns?

The default seller cadences are written in an honest, no-pressure voice — they lead with market data and helpful context, not "list with me now." That's what gets long-timeline sellers (6-24 months out) to actually stay on the list and eventually call.

Can I customize the outreach for specific towns or price bands?

Yes. You can split cadences by town, price band, seller type, and lead source. Farming a specific ZIP code with luxury sellers looks completely different from working expireds under $500K — and the workflow supports both without you having to build a separate CRM for each.

Does it work if I don't have a big database yet?

Yes. The workflows scale from "starting from zero" to "10,000 contacts." New home-valuation leads, expireds, and FSBOs flow into the cadences from day one, and past-client sequences activate as your database grows.

How is it different from ISA-driven seller lead services?

ISA services rent you appointments — you own nothing after you cancel. Seller Workflows build your own owned pipeline: your database, your relationships, your consistent presence in front of your farm. When you stop paying, you keep everything you built.
