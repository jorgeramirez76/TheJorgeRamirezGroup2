Built for Real Estate Agents, Not Generic Call Centers

Real estate leads don't behave like SaaS leads. A buyer who inquires about a Zillow listing at 9:47pm doesn't want a voicemail — they're already texting three other agents. A seller filling out a home valuation form wants to know what their house is worth, right now, from someone who sounds like they know the local market. Generic AI call bots miss on both counts because they don't understand the transaction, the objections, or the pace.

AI Voice was trained on the actual conversations real estate agents have every day — buyer qualification, seller motivation, timeline discovery, financing check, showing scheduling, and the "I'm just looking" wall. It handles them the way a good ISA would, in your voice, at every hour a lead comes in.

How Real Estate Agents Use It

1. New buyer lead from Zillow, Realtor.com, or your site — AI calls in your voice within 60 seconds, references the property they viewed, and asks about timeline, price range, and financing.

2. Seller lead from a home valuation form — AI calls back the same day, confirms property details, asks about motivation and timeline, and books the CMA appointment on your calendar.

3. Old database reactivation — AI works through leads that went cold 6, 12, 18 months ago and re-qualifies them. The ones who are ready now get booked. The rest go back into nurture.

4. After-hours + weekend coverage — the AI picks up every call your business line misses on nights, weekends, and while you're in a listing appointment. No lead sits until morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can it handle bilingual (English/Spanish) leads?
Yes. The AI detects the caller's language on the first response and continues the entire conversation natively. Both language flows use the same cloned voice, so it still sounds like you.

What happens when the lead wants specific home price info?
The AI can quote MLS-sourced data if you connect your IDX or MLS feed. If a question goes beyond that — market forecast, negotiation strategy, seller financing — the AI schedules a call with you or transfers live.

Does it work with my existing CRM?
Yes. AI Voice writes back to AI Sales Pipeline natively, and integrates with Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Sierra, Chime, and other real estate CRMs via API. Every call logs a full transcript to the lead record.

Is this legal in NJ / my state?
The system respects state-level do-not-call lists, discloses recording where required, and honors NJ Real Estate Commission rules on solicitation. Your compliance officer can review the full disclosure flow before you go live.
