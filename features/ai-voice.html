And you can't be on the phone at 9pm when a Zillow inquiry hits, or between showings, or while you're driving to a listing appointment.

AI Voice fixes the speed-to-lead problem without hiring an ISA. How It Works

1. Lead trigger fires — A new inquiry lands in your CRM from any source: your website, IDX, Facebook lead ads, portals, or a missed call to your business line.

2. AI dials in under 60 seconds — Your voice agent calls the lead in your cloned voice, references the property or search they inquired about, and opens with a natural greeting — not a script.

3. Qualifying conversation happens — Timeline, price range, financing status, motivation. The AI listens, adapts, handles common objections, and knows when to stop qualifying and start booking.

4. Appointment gets booked — Live to your calendar with real availability, or scheduled callback slot if the lead prefers. Full recording and transcript pushed into the lead's CRM record for your review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it actually sound like me?
Yes. We use short samples of your voice — usually a five-minute recording is enough — to build the clone. Most people can't tell it's AI on the first call. You approve the voice before it ever dials a lead.

What happens if the lead asks something the AI can't answer?
The AI is trained to say "let me get you connected with Jorge on that" and either warm-transfer you live or schedule a callback. It never invents information about a property, price, or your services.

Is this TCPA compliant?
Yes. The system honors do-not-call lists, respects quiet hours by time zone, logs consent, and offers opt-out on every call. Every conversation is recorded and stored with proper retention.

Can I hear a live call before I sign up?
Yes. The demo includes a live outbound call to a number you provide, using a sample voice, so you hear the quality before deciding.
