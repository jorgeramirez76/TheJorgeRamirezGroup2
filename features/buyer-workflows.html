The Buyer Lead Problem

Most buyer leads don't die because they're bad. They die because nobody worked them consistently for the 30, 60, or 90 days it takes for a curious browser to become a signed buyer's agent client. You get 20 new inquiries in a week, respond to the two that seem hottest, and the other 18 get one email, maybe a text, then silence. Three months later they buy with someone else — usually the agent who kept showing up.

Buyer Workflows are the automation layer that keeps every buyer lead getting the right message at the right time — from the first inquiry through mortgage pre-approval, through active tour scheduling, through offer, through contract-to-close — without you having to remember who is where in the process.

How It Works

1. Every new buyer lead enters an intake sequence. Within minutes of hitting your pipeline, the lead gets a personal first-touch (text + email), an offer to book time on your calendar, and a short qualifying quiz that captures budget, timeline, town preferences, and mortgage status.

2. The pipeline scores and routes. Answers feed a lead score. Hot leads (pre-approved, 0-3 month timeline, specific towns) get pushed to your top-of-inbox and trigger an AI Clone Video. Warmer leads go to a 30-day nurture. Long-timeline leads go to a monthly market-update cadence so you own the relationship when they're ready.

3. Tour readiness is tracked automatically. When a lead clicks three listings in a 48-hour window, opens a mortgage-calculator page, or replies with intent language, the workflow flags them "tour-ready" and prompts you (or your ISA) to schedule a showing that day.

4. Every touch, timed and logged. Day-of, day-2, day-7, day-14, day-30, day-60 — the sequence runs on its own, but every message shows up in your inbox as a threaded conversation so you can jump in the moment a lead replies.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a regular drip campaign?

A drip campaign sends the same messages to everyone on a fixed schedule. Buyer Workflows adapt — the sequence a hot, pre-approved lead sees is different from what a long-timeline lead sees. Behavior (listing views, replies, calendar clicks) actively reshapes the cadence in real time.

Do the automated messages feel spammy?

The default sequences are written in an honest, no-pressure voice — closer to how a good agent actually texts a buyer than to typical drip copy. Every message is fully editable, and AI Clone Video adds a personal on-camera touch at key moments.

Can my ISA or assistant run this without me?

Yes. You can assign inbox seats to team members, route hot leads to specific agents, and let an ISA handle qualification while you take over the moment a lead is tour-ready. Everything is logged so the handoff is clean.

Does it integrate with the lead sources I already use?

Yes. Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, KW Command, portal-integrated form submissions, IDX inquiries, Facebook lead ads, and CSV imports all flow in. New leads enter the workflow automatically no matter which source they came from.
