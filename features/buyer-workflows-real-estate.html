Real Estate Buyer Leads Are Only Worth What You Follow Up

Every real estate agent has the same problem with buyer leads: too many of them, too little time, and a follow-up window measured in minutes. A Zillow lead that gets a response inside 5 minutes converts at multiples of one that gets a response in 24 hours. And most of your buyer pool isn't buying this weekend — they're buying in 60 or 90 days, and the agent they end up hiring is the one who stayed in touch consistently in the meantime.

Buyer Workflows are purpose-built for the way real estate agents actually work a buyer pipeline: instant response on the first touch, real qualification without a phone-tag marathon, tour scheduling on autopilot, and a nurture that keeps you in the conversation for as long as it takes them to be ready.

How Real Estate Agents Use It

1. New Zillow / Realtor.com inquiry. Within 60 seconds, the lead gets a personalized text and email from you, plus a short intake link that captures budget, timeline, and pre-approval status. You get a Slack-style push notification only for the leads that actually qualify.

2. Tour-ready detection. A lead views three listings, opens a mortgage calculator, or replies "when can we see it" — the workflow flags them and drops a "let's get on the calendar" text with a live booking link, so the showing gets set without back-and-forth.

3. Long-timeline nurture. The lead says "6-12 months out." Instead of dropping them, the workflow puts them on a monthly market-update cadence with new listings in their target towns and a check-in from you every 30-45 days. When they get serious, you're the agent they already know.

4. Contract-to-close. Once a buyer is under contract, a new cadence handles inspection reminders, mortgage-milestone check-ins, and a closing-day countdown so nothing gets dropped between attorney review and CTC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this replace my current CRM (Follow Up Boss, KW Command, Sierra Interactive)?

Most agents run AI Sales Pipeline as their primary lead-work layer — the place where SMS, email, video, and cadences all live. You can keep your existing CRM as a system of record if you want to, and sync the two, or move everything over. Onboarding covers a migration path either way.

What kind of response time does the first-touch text actually hit?

The instant-response sequence fires within 60 seconds of a lead entering the pipeline, 24/7. That includes weekends and after-hours (unless you enable quiet-hours), which is where most agents lose portal leads today.

Can it handle a bilingual buyer pool?

Yes. Sequences ship in both English and Spanish, and the pipeline auto-routes based on lead-preferred language. Especially strong for NJ, NY, TX, FL, and CA markets with significant Latino buyer demand.

Is texting compliant with TCPA?

Yes. Every message respects consent, opt-out language, quiet-hours, and A2P 10DLC registration. You control which lead sources are eligible for SMS and can exclude any contact from automated outreach entirely.
