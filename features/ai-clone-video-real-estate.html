How Real Estate Agents Use It

1. New buyer inquiry. A lead requests info on your Summit listing. Within minutes, they get a personal video from you: "Hey Marcus — saw you asked about the Springfield Avenue place. Here's a quick walk-through of what makes it special. Text me back if you want to see it this weekend."

Frequently Asked Questions

Do buyers and sellers actually respond to AI video?

Yes — when the message is specific. A video that names the lead, references the town they're shopping in, and mentions the exact property or price band feels dramatically more personal than a form email. Agents on the platform consistently see higher reply rates than their SMS or email drip sequences.

Can I use this for Spanish-speaking clients?

Yes. You can train a Spanish clone of your voice (or a bilingual clone that switches between English and Spanish based on the lead's preferred language). This is especially useful for agents serving Latino buyers and sellers in NJ, NY, TX, FL, and CA markets.

Does it work with my existing CRM data?

AI Sales Pipeline includes a full CRM, but the AI Clone Video workflow can also pull merge data from imported lead lists, Zillow, Realtor.com, KW Command, Follow Up Boss exports, and Sierra Interactive. You keep your lead flow — we make it personal.

What about NAR and MLS advertising rules?

Every message you send is your message — you control the script, the disclosures, the brokerage attribution, and the licensing info in the signature. Templates ship with standard broker-approved disclosure language and can be customized to match your compliance office.
