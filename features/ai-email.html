The Problem: Generic Drip Campaigns Don't Convert Anymore

The old playbook was simple: dump every new lead into a 7-email drip, send the same messages to everyone, hope for a reply. That playbook is dead. Modern buyers have inbox fatigue. They can smell a template in the subject line. They unsubscribe or, worse, mark you as spam — which nukes your sender reputation and hurts every future send. Meanwhile the leads who would have engaged with a genuinely relevant email never get one, because your CRM couldn't tell the difference between a hot buyer and a research-phase browser.

AI Email is built to fix all three problems: it writes each email fresh based on the lead's actual behavior, it adapts the sequence in real time based on opens and clicks, and it stops sending the moment a lead loses interest instead of hammering them into a spam complaint.

How It Works

Unlike traditional email marketing, AI Email doesn't send the same template to everyone. It learns from each lead's behavior and customizes their entire sequence.

1. Lead arrives — They visit your site or inquire about a property

2. AI reads intent — What did they search? What property? First-time buyer or investor?

3. Sequence created — Your AI builds a unique sequence for THIS lead

4. Email 1 drops — Personalized subject, personalized content

5. AI monitors & adapts — They didn't open? Email 2 is different. They clicked a link? Email 3 shifts focus.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from Mailchimp or a normal drip tool?
Traditional drip tools send the same email to everyone on the same schedule. AI Email writes each email fresh for each lead, decides when to send based on when that specific person is likely to open, and changes the next email based on what the lead did with the last one. It's per-lead, not per-list.

Will my emails end up in spam?
Deliverability is a first-class concern. The system uses proper authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), warms sending domains gradually, respects engagement signals, and stops sending to leads showing spam-complaint risk. Because every email is unique and behavior-triggered, the "template spam" pattern that hurts drip campaigns doesn't apply.

Can I approve emails before they send?
Yes. You can run in fully automated mode, in a review-first mode where every email drops into your approval queue, or in a hybrid where high-value leads get review and the rest go automatic. Choose what fits your comfort level.

Does it integrate with my existing email address and CRM?
Yes. AI Email sends from your own domain (jorge@yourdomain.com — not a shared IP) and syncs bidirectionally with AI Sales Pipeline, HubSpot, Salesforce, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, and most major CRMs. Replies land in your inbox and the CRM record at the same time.
