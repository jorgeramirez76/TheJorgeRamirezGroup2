Real Estate Listings Come From Persistent Follow-Up

In real estate, listings are the compounding asset. One listing generates the buyer leads for two or three more transactions, the sign in the yard, the "just sold" postcard, and the referral pipeline for years. But listings don't come from a single email — they come from being the agent who followed up for six months while the seller decided. Most agents give up after two touches. Seller Workflows are how you become the one that doesn't.

Purpose-built for the way real estate agents earn listings: home-valuation leads worked instantly, expired listings hit within 24 hours, FSBOs nurtured weekly until they call, and a past-client database that stays warm forever.

How Real Estate Agents Use It

1. Home-valuation lead on your site. A homeowner in your farm requests a value. Within minutes they get an accurate AVM range, a personal AI Clone Video from you explaining what's actually happening in their neighborhood, and an offer to meet — no pressure, no commitment. The cadence then continues weekly then monthly for as long as it takes.

2. Expired listing from your MLS feed. The workflow pulls new expireds daily, matches them to your farm, and starts a multi-touch cadence: same-day text, day-2 personal video, day-5 handwritten-letter task, day-14 case-study email — with messaging tuned to why their previous agent's listing didn't move.

3. FSBO in your target ZIP. New For-Sale-By-Owner listings trigger a 60-90 day nurture with market updates, pricing insights, and a warm "let me know how it's going" tone. When they're ready to hire an agent, you're the name they already know.

4. Past-client database on autopilot. Anniversary check-ins, annual home-value reports, "just sold in your neighborhood" updates, and quarterly market notes keep the database that produces your referrals warm — without any manual work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the FSBO and expired messaging feel pushy?

The default cadences are written in the same honest, no-pressure voice you'd want an agent using with your own family — leading with market context and useful data instead of "list with me now." That's exactly why long-timeline sellers stay engaged instead of blocking your number.

Does it work with my MLS?

The workflow supports most major MLS feeds and can ingest expired and FSBO data through the standard IDX / RETS / Spark API channels used across the country. Onboarding covers the specific setup for your board.

Can I use this for a Spanish-speaking seller database?

Yes. Sequences ship in both English and Spanish, and the pipeline routes contacts by their preferred language. Especially strong for agents serving Latino sellers in NJ, NY, TX, FL, and CA who want a real Spanish-language cadence — not machine-translated boilerplate.
