The Problem

Most agents run Facebook and Instagram ads to strangers. Cold audiences, cold copy, cold clicks — and cost-per-lead numbers north of $25 that never convert. Meanwhile, the people who already visited your website, watched your listing video, or opened three of your emails? They're the ones actually thinking about buying or selling — and they see nothing from you after they leave your site. Facebook Retargeting inside AI Sales Pipeline flips that. Instead of shouting at strangers, you show ads only to people who already raised a hand.

How It Works

  1. Pixel + CRM sync. The Facebook Pixel installs on your website and IDX in under 15 minutes. It quietly tracks who visits, what listings they view, and which pages they revisit — and syncs that with your CRM contacts.
  2. Custom audiences build themselves. The system automatically builds warm audiences: "home valuation form abandoners," "listing viewers in the last 14 days," "opened 3+ emails but didn't reply," etc.
  3. Retargeting ads run to warm-only. Instagram and Facebook feed ads run only to those warm audiences — never to cold, general demographic targets.
  4. Warm leads come back cheaper. Because these people already know you, click rates are 8-15% and cost-per-lead lands in the $2-5 range instead of $25-40.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a big Facebook ad budget to make this work?

No. Retargeting works efficiently at $10-20/day because your audience is small and warm. Most agents start at $300-500/month and scale up as they see cost-per-lead drop below cold-ad benchmarks.

Does this work with iOS 14+ privacy changes?

Yes. AI Sales Pipeline uses the Conversions API in addition to the Pixel, which sends server-side signals directly to Facebook. That's the current best practice for accurate retargeting after Apple's tracking changes.

Can I run ads without hiring a Facebook ads manager?

Yes. The pre-built ad templates and audience automation are designed to be launched directly by the agent. If you want expert help, our team can set up your first three campaigns as part of onboarding.

Will this comply with fair housing rules?

Yes. The system uses Facebook's Special Ad Category for Housing, which restricts age, gender, ZIP-code, and demographic targeting per HUD guidelines. Retargeting to your own warm audiences is compliant because you're targeting people based on their behavior on your own site, not protected demographic categories.
