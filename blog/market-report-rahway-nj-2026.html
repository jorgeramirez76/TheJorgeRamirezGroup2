A reproducible comparison method

  1. Name the geography first: Rahway City, Union County, or one property.
  2. Write down the publisher, table or report name, release period, selected category, and access date.
  3. Compare identical field labels only. Keep an average separate from a median and a survey estimate separate from a transaction.
  4. Use municipal and DCA records to investigate local property or construction questions; do not turn permit activity into listing inventory.
  5. Use current comparable properties and property details for a CMA; do not substitute a municipality or county figure.
