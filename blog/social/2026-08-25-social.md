### Instagram Caption

NJ home buyers: check these spots at EVERY showing before you make an offer. 🏠🔍

Most people look at kitchens and master baths. The real condition of a NJ home is in these places:

🏚️ BASEMENT: Open the door and smell it first. A musty, earthy smell = established mold. Then look at:
- Floor joists (dark staining, fuzzy growth)
- Foundation walls (white chalky deposits = water infiltration history)
- Whether a dehumidifier is running constantly

🚰 UNDER THE SINK: Open it. Look for dark staining, soft wood, or visible growth on the cabinet floor.

🚿 BATHROOM WALLS: Press gently near the shower base. Soft drywall = water has been getting behind it.

🔧 CEILING STAINS: Water stains near exterior walls or below a bathroom = past or ongoing leak.

🕯️ HEAVILY SCENTED HOME: Candles and diffusers everywhere during a showing warrants extra nose-testing in low-traffic areas.

Ask directly: "Has the seller had mold remediation done in the past 5 years?"

NJ sellers are legally required to disclose known material defects. If they're asked directly and don't answer, that tells you something too.
