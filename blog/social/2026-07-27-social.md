## POST 2: Most NJ Homeowners Set Up Their Homes Backward — And It's Costing Them at the Closing Table

---

### Facebook Post

Here's something I see in NJ homes every single week: sellers list their homes with furniture arranged the exact same way they've been living for the past eight years. And that's costing them money.

How you set up your home to live in it and how you set it up to sell it are completely different things. The most common mistakes I see across Union, Essex, and Morris County homes:

❌ All furniture pushed against the walls (makes rooms feel smaller, not bigger)
❌ Rugs that are too small for the seating group
❌ Furniture blocking windows and cutting off natural light
❌ No clear 3-foot traffic path through rooms
❌ Too much furniture competing for the same space

Every one of these is free to fix. And fixing them before your listing photos are taken can be worth $10,000–$25,000 on a mid-range NJ home.
