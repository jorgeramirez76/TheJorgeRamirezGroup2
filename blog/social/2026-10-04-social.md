### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook sent a home hack this week about the most abused pan in the kitchen: the scorched baking sheet. Baking soda, hydrogen peroxide, a couple of hours and a non-scratch pad, and a pan you were about to toss looks close to new.

I bring it up because the habit behind it is how you get a kitchen ready to sell. Buyers in Westfield, Summit and Montclair open the oven and the drawer under it. Scorched pans, carbon on the oven floor and a greasy hood filter add up to "this kitchen needs work," and that lands on the offer.

Restore before you replace. Soak the oven racks in the same paste. Run the hood filter through the dishwasher. Bring the stainless sink back with baking soda and a soft pad. Save the replacing for what does not work.

✅ Range hood filter: through the dishwasher
✅ Stainless sink: baking soda and a soft pad, then a drop of oil
✅ Cabinet pulls: degrease and tighten
🚫 A burner that will not light is a repair or a disclosure, not a cleaning job
