## Post 3: Most NJ Homeowners Have Their Rooms Set Up Backward

### Facebook Post
Here's something nobody tells NJ home sellers: most furniture in a family home is arranged for the people who live there — not for buyers who are evaluating the space. The two setups are almost always different.

Sofa pushed against the wall → makes the room feel smaller, not bigger.
Area rug too small → makes furniture look heavy, room look cheap.
Furniture blocking the window → kills the natural light buyers are paying for.
Bed under the window → photographs terribly and signals a layout problem.

These are the room setup mistakes that make buyers feel "something's off" in a showing — even when they can't say exactly what it is. And they all affect offer price.
