Your living room is the first major impression NJ buyers get — and most sellers are setting it up wrong. ⬇️

Here are the 9 arrangement changes that make buyers feel at home the moment they walk in:

1. Float furniture away from walls (8–12 inches minimum)
2. Face seating toward each other — not just the TV
3. Keep seating within 8 feet for conversation
4. Anchor with a properly sized area rug
5. Add a secondary seating zone if space allows
6. Coffee table 16–18 inches from the sofa
7. Remove furniture that crowds traffic paths
8. Layer warm lighting (2700K bulbs throughout)
9. Clear sightlines from entry to far wall

None of this requires buying anything new. It requires moving what you have into the right arrangement — and that's the difference between a buyer who offers and a buyer who keeps looking.
