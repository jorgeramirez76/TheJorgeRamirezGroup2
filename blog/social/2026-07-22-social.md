## Article 2: The Kitchen Storage Mistake Hurting Your NJ Home Sale
**Blog post:** [kitchen-plastic-storage-nj-home-sellers-2026.html](https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/kitchen-plastic-storage-nj-home-sellers-2026)

---

### Facebook Post

Buyers open every cabinet in an NJ home showing. Every. Single. One. I've watched it happen at hundreds of listings across Union and Essex County over 15 years.

What they find inside shapes their impression of how the home was maintained. And one of the most consistent showing negatives I see? Pantries full of stained, warped plastic containers. Orange tomato residue. Yellowed turmeric tint. Cloudy lids that haven't sealed properly in three years.

Here's what most sellers don't know: seven types of food permanently damage plastic storage containers. Tomato sauce (the beta-carotene bonds to the plastic surface — it doesn't wash out). Turmeric and curry (the most aggressive stainer in any kitchen). Coffee and tea tannins. Red wine. Cooking oils. Hot liquids poured in while still steaming. Acidic citrus juice.

Before listing, pull everything out of your pantry, discard anything stained or warped, replace with a uniform set of clean containers, and organize by category with visible empty space. The cost is $30-$60. In the context of your NJ home sale, that's one of the highest-ROI prep decisions you'll make.
