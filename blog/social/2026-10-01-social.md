**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/hide-clutter-in-plain-sight-nj-home-sellers-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/05-kitchen.webp

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

You do not have to empty your house to sell it. House Outlook looked at how professional organizers hide clutter in plain sight, and the three habits they found are a staging playbook for New Jersey sellers.

One: color-block your storage. When every bin and canister on a shelf is the same color, the eye reads one object instead of twelve. Two: curate the kitchen counter so the mugs, boards and gadgets that stay out share a palette. Three: group items on open shelves and leave real space between the groups.

Why it matters when you are listing: buyers do not see clutter as mess. They see it as a house without enough storage, and they price that in. These tricks hide the volume without taking away the things you use every week.

## Article 2: The Rule of Three: The Designer Habit That Makes NJ Listing Photos Look Finished

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/rule-of-three-decorating-nj-listing-photos-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/04-brick-stone.webp
**Instagram image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/04-brick-stone.webp

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Designers arrange things in threes, and House Outlook just explained why: the eye settles on an odd number, and three is interesting without being busy. The recipe is one tall item, one low item, one sculptural piece. A lamp, a stack of books, a bowl.

For a New Jersey seller, the rule is a way to stop guessing before listing photos. Entry console: lamp, key bowl, plant. Kitchen counter: board, bowl of lemons, utensil crock. Nightstands: lamp, book, one object, and both sides match. Over the bed: one large piece or three small ones.

The part most sellers miss: three is the maximum, not the minimum. Shop your own house before you buy a single thing, and fix the paint and repairs first. A styled nightstand does not rescue a stained ceiling.
