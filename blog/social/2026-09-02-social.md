## Post 3: Appliance Maintenance That Protects Your NJ Sale Price

**Facebook (100-150 words):**

Want to know what shows up on almost every NJ home inspection report? Appliances. The HVAC filter that hasn't been changed in 8 months. The dryer vent packed with lint (flagged as a fire hazard). The dishwasher that smells because the filter's never been cleaned.

Each one of those items is a bullet point in the buyer's request-for-credit letter. Individually they're $100 fixes. On an inspection report they become $500 negotiating weapons.

I tell every NJ seller the same thing: the inspection isn't a test, it's a negotiation document. Every item you can eliminate before listing is leverage you're protecting.
