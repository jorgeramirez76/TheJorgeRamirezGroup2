## POST 2: 12 Design Mistakes That Make Your NJ Home Look Cheap to Buyers

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Most sellers focus on the kitchen and bathrooms. But buyers are already judging your home before they get there.

Here are the design mistakes I see most often in NJ homes that quietly cost sellers money at the negotiating table:

❌ Cool white light bulbs (use 2700K warm white instead — $40 fix)
❌ Curtain rods mounted at window height (move them to ceiling height)
❌ Area rugs that are too small for the room
❌ Matched furniture sets from a single big-box store
❌ Dated or builder-grade light fixtures
❌ Open shelving overloaded with stuff
❌ Bold or dated paint colors
❌ Cheap or mismatched bathroom hardware

12 mistakes. Most cost under $300 each to fix. Collectively they can add $15,000–$40,000 to your NJ sale price.

## POST 3: 2026 Interior Design Trends: What NJ Home Sellers Need to Know

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

2026's biggest interior design trends are shifting fast — and if you're selling your NJ home this summer, some of them work in your favor and some of them don't.

The trends that HELP your NJ sale:
✅ Warm, layered lighting (swap your cool bulbs for 2700K warm white)
✅ Dimensional neutrals — warm whites and greige over flat cool gray
✅ Dark wood as contrast: walnut tones, medium-dark floors
✅ Classic architectural details: paneling, wainscoting, built-ins

The trends to SKIP when staging:
❌ Maximalism and curated collections
❌ Nostalgic patterns and bold saturated colors
❌ Anything that requires buyers to share your specific taste

2026 buyers want warm, intentional, and move-in ready — not a magazine shoot that reflects only your style.
