## Article 1: The Paint Colors That Hurt Your Home’s Value (and What NJ Buyers Respond To)

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/paint-colors-hurt-home-value-nj-sellers-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/06-bedroom.webp

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook asked two industry experts which paint colors sell homes faster and which ones send buyers running. Their answers match what I see in New Jersey listings every week.

The colors that cost sellers money: dark, saturated walls in small rooms, bright kids'-room colors, early-2000s yellow-beige in every room, red dining rooms, cold blue-white in rooms with no sun, dark ceilings, and three different whites on the trim. None of it lowers an appraisal. It shrinks the photos and hands buyers a repainting number to subtract from the offer.

The colors that help: warm white, soft greige, pale gray-green, muted blue in the bath, and one consistent trim white through the whole house.

You do not need to repaint everything. Rank the rooms by what they cost you in the photos, price the top three against the whole house, and spend there.

## Article 2: Selling a NJ Home With Pets: The Cleaning Routine That Keeps Buyers From Noticing

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/selling-nj-home-with-pets-cleaning-showings-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/blog-hero/families-2026-1280.webp
**Instagram image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/blog-hero/families-2026-1280.webp

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Most buyers say they love dogs. Almost none of them want to smell yours.

House Outlook rounded up the cleaning products pet owners cannot live without: enzyme cleaner, a rubber brush for hair, a pet-hair vacuum, an odor absorber, washable covers and a carpet stain remover. That is a good kit for living with a pet. Selling with one is a different job, because the goal is a house where a buyer with no pets cannot tell one lives there.

The pre-listing pass: find every old accident with a UV flashlight and treat it with enzyme, wash everything soft, HEPA vacuum floors and furniture, change the HVAC filter, repair scratched doors instead of hiding them, and move the crate, bowls and litter box out of the photos. On showing day, take the dog with you, open the windows, and skip the scented candles. Buyers read fragrance as a cover-up.
