## POST 3: Home Systems That Age Out on NJ Sellers

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Before your NJ home goes on the market, you need to know four numbers:
→ Age of your HVAC
→ Age of your water heater
→ Age of your roof
→ Amperage on your electrical panel

These four systems show up in almost every NJ home inspection. And when they're old, they become the biggest credit conversations in the transaction.

Here are the cutoffs buyers and inspectors apply:
🌡️ A/C over 10–12 years → flagged
💧 Water heater over 10 years → credit conversation
🏠 Roof over 20 years → insurance and lender issues
⚡ 100-amp panel → EV charging concern above $700K

Know your numbers before buyers do. Decide in advance: replace it, price around it, or plan a credit. Pretending it isn't there is the only wrong answer.
