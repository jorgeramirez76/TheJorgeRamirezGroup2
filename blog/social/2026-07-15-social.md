## Article 3: Kitchen Details NJ Buyers Notice

**URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/kitchen-details-nj-buyers-notice-2026

---

### Facebook Post

NJ buyers spend more time in the kitchen than any other room during showings — and they look at things you haven't thought about in years.

Cabinet doors that don't close flush. The under-sink cabinet (water stains = old leak = buyer anxiety). A range hood that makes noise but moves no air. An oven interior that signals general neglect. Missing GFCI outlets (a very common finding in pre-1990 NJ homes). A dripping faucet. A garbage disposal that hums but doesn't spin.

None of these will individually kill a sale. Together, they paint a picture: "this seller has been living here rather than maintaining here." That perception transfers to every system the buyer can't see.

I've watched a $900K Westfield Colonial lose $30,000 in final price because of kitchen details that a Saturday of repairs would have fixed. The full checklist — what to look for, what to fix, and what each item costs — is on the blog.
