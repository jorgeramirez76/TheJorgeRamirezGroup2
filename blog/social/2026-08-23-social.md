Most NJ sellers spend money staging their home in the wrong order.

They buy the throw pillows. They get the decorative trays. They add the candles. And the room still doesn't look right. 🤔

Here's why: they skipped steps 1 and 2.

The correct staging order is:

1️⃣ Space planning — where does the furniture actually go? How does traffic flow? What's the focal point?

2️⃣ Paint, floors, and area rugs — this is the room's backdrop. It sets the emotional tone. Cool gray walls in 2026 are a problem.

3️⃣ Accessories and decor — LAST. Not first. Once the structure is right, you don't need much.

A Cranford seller I worked with spent $2,200 on a repaint and furniture rearrangement before touching a single accessory. Went under contract in 8 days with two offers.
