### Instagram Caption
10 home safety warning signs NJ inspectors find every week — most sellers have no idea they're there:

🔴 Fishy smell near outlets or switches = overheating wiring (electrical fire risk)
🔴 Light switch that feels warm to the touch = overloaded or failing circuit
🔴 Outlets within 6 feet of a sink without GFCI protection = code violation
🔴 Yellow or orange gas flame = incomplete combustion, possible CO production
🔴 Missing or expired CO detectors = NJ closing requirement (deals get delayed)
🔡 Smoke detectors over 10 years old = safety deficiency, flagged on every report
🟡 Dryer venting into attic or crawlspace = fire and moisture risk
🟡 Federal Pacific or Zinsco electrical panel = major buyer red flag
🟡 Extension cords used as permanent wiring = documented inspection finding
🟡 Double-tapped breakers in the panel = fire risk, requires electrician

Every one of these that a buyer's inspector finds is a line item for negotiation. Most cost under $300 to fix. None of them should be on your inspection report when there's a deal on the table.
