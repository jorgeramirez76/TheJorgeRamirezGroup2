## Article 3: Pre-Listing Home Inspection in NJ: Why Sellers Are Doing It First
**URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/pre-listing-inspection-nj-sellers-2026

### Facebook Post (100–150 words)
Here's a shift I'm seeing more often from experienced NJ sellers: they order the home inspection themselves, before listing—not after. And it's not counterintuitive once you think it through. The buyer's inspector is going to find whatever is in your house. The question is whether you find out on your schedule or during attorney review with a live deal at risk. A pre-listing inspection lets you address issues on your terms, get multiple contractor quotes without deadline pressure, and go to market with documentation in hand. When the buyer's inspector finds the same things, it's not a surprise—it's a disclosed, documented condition. That changes the negotiation completely.
