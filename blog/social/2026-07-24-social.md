## POST 2: Clean Your House in the Right Order

**Article:** The Correct Order to Clean Your House Before NJ Home Showings
**URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/cleaning-house-order-nj-sellers-2026

---

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Here's a mistake I see NJ sellers make constantly: they vacuum the carpets, then dust the ceiling fans, then watch the debris settle on the freshly vacuumed floor.

Cleaning order matters when you're selling — especially when buyers in NJ can request a showing with one hour's notice.

The correct sequence: declutter first. Dust from high surfaces down. Wipe horizontal surfaces. Clean bathrooms as a contained unit. Kitchen last before floors. Vacuum. Mop hard floors and exit through the garage.

That order means you never re-dirty a room you already finished. And it gets a standard NJ colonial to show-ready in under 90 minutes from a daily maintenance baseline.

## POST 3: The Basket System for NJ Families Selling Their Home

**Article:** The Basket System That Keeps NJ Homes Show-Ready With Kids and Pets
**URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/home-basket-system-nj-sellers-2026

---

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Selling a home with kids and a dog in NJ — where buyers can request a showing with one hour's notice — is genuinely hard. The families who do it well have a system.

The simplest one I've seen work: one basket per person. When a showing notification hits, everyone grabs their basket and sweeps their personal items out of the common areas. Baskets go into bedrooms. Bedroom doors close. The common areas look staged because nothing personal is in them.

Combine with a pre-listing storage purge (30-40% of belongings into temporary storage before the listing goes live) and a practiced 10-minute drill, and you can go from everyday family life to show-ready in under 10 minutes.
