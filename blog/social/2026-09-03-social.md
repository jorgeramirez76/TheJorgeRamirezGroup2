### Facebook (100–150 words)

Here's something I see in almost every pre-listing walkthrough I do: a garage that's working against the seller.

Buyers walk through every room — and that includes the garage. When they step into a stuffed, oil-stained, dimly lit garage, two things happen: they mentally subtract storage from the home's value, and they start wondering what else hasn't been taken care of.

The fix costs $300–$500 in most NJ homes. The cost of skipping it? Potentially thousands in buyer concessions at closing.

New post covers the 6 garage fixes I tell every NJ seller to make before listing: clearing the floor, addressing oil stains, the fire door between the garage and house, garage door auto-reverse, lighting, and organization.
