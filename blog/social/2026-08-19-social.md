## Post 2: NJ Property Tax Appeal Guide 2026

### Facebook Post
New Jersey has the highest property taxes in the country. But that doesn't mean you have to pay more than your fair share.

Every year, thousands of NJ homeowners are over-assessed — paying taxes on an implied home value that's $50K, $75K, or even $100K higher than what comparable homes actually sell for. The appeal process exists for exactly this reason. Most people don't use it because they don't know where to start.

Here's the short version:
📅 Deadline: April 1 (mark your calendar)
🏠 What you need: Comparable sales showing the assessor's implied value is too high
📋 Where you file: Your County Board of Taxation

A successful appeal can save the average NJ homeowner $2,000-$4,000 per year. Over five years, that's real money. Read the full step-by-step guide on the blog — link in bio.
