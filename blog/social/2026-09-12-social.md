## Article 1: The "Five Things" Method: How Overwhelmed NJ Sellers Get Show-Ready Fast

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

A showing request comes in for 5:30. It's 4:15. The living room looks like a family lives in it — because one does. And you freeze.

Every seller I've worked with in Westfield, Maplewood, and Morristown knows that feeling. The fix I now teach before every listing goes live is the "Five Things" method from therapist KC Davis. Every messy room contains only five kinds of things: trash, laundry, dishes, things that have a home, and things that don't. Five passes through the room, one category per pass. No decisions, no overwhelm.

It gets a staged NJ home from lived-in to show-ready in about 40 minutes — and it's sustainable for the full 30 to 60 days you're on the market. That consistency is what protects your price.
