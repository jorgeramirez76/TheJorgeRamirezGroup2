## Article 2: Cooking While Your NJ Home Is Listed: Fast Meals, a Clean Stove and Showing-Day Timing

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/cooking-while-home-listed-nj-sellers-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/blog-hero/value-2026-1280.webp
**Instagram image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/blog-hero/value-2026-1280.webp

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Your house is listed for weeks, and the household still eats dinner. Buyers judge the kitchen harder than any room, and a stove used at 6 p.m. tells on you at a 7 p.m. showing.

House Outlook's hack this week was a shortcut for caramelized onions: a pinch of baking soda speeds the browning, so they finish in a fraction of the time. The seller's lesson is the pattern. Less stove time means less steam, less splatter and less smell for the next buyer to walk into.

While your NJ home is on the market: sheet-pan and one-pot meals, the grill while the weather holds, no fish or bacon on showing days, run the hood and check that it vents outside, and finish a full kitchen reset within thirty minutes of dinner. Inspectors in Westfield and Morristown check the hood too.
