## Article 2: Dents in Your Hardwood Floors? What NJ Sellers Should Fix, Leave Alone and Disclose Before Listing

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/hardwood-floor-dents-fix-before-listing-nj-home-sellers-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/editorial/nj-property-tax-research-2026-1280.webp (crop to 1200×630)
**Instagram image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/editorial/nj-property-tax-research-2026-1280.webp (crop to 1080×1080)

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook linked an article this week on fixing dents in hardwood floors, with the honest admission that no number of rugs or felt pads prevents them. True. Every colonial in Montclair, Maplewood and Cranford with original oak has dents, and buyers expect some.

Before listing, walk every room in daylight with the rugs rolled back and put each mark in a column. Leave the patina alone. Steam or fill the handful of deep dents in the traffic path. If the finish is dull and worn thin by the sink, a screen and recoat refreshes it in a day. A full sand only pays when the floor is holding the price down.

And press on every mark with your heel. If it gives, that is water, not a dent, and it is a disclosure conversation. Do not buy new rugs to cover it. Buyers lift them. Full guide: thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/hardwood-floor-dents-fix-before-listing-nj-home-sellers-2026

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook linked a list this week of seventeen ways to use a three-tier rolling cart when the pantry and cabinets are full. The carts work. I have one in my garage.

But I have walked buyers through condos in Jersey City and capes in Scotch Plains where a loaded cart at the end of the counter said the one thing a seller never wants a buyer to think: there is not enough storage here. It does not matter that the cart is tidy. It is storage that did not fit inside the storage.

My rules: zero or one cart in the photos, and that one is a vignette, a bar cart or a coffee station, not inventory. Use another as the showing-day kit that rolls to the garage when an appointment is confirmed. And if the cart is holding pantry food, the fix is in the pantry.
