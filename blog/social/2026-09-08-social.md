## Post 1: The Fall Home Prep Checklist NJ Sellers Need Before Listing in 2026

### Facebook Post (100–150 words)

September is one of the best times to sell a home in NJ — but only if your home is ready for fall. Serious buyers are still active, there's less competition than spring, and motivated families want to be settled before the holidays. But fall prep is different from spring prep.

The checklist I walk through with every seller before a fall listing:
✅ Clean the gutters (clogged gutters = inspection issues)
✅ Service the heating system before the inspector tests it
✅ Reseal exterior gaps and caulk around windows
✅ Upgrade lighting for shorter fall days
✅ Maintain curb appeal through the listing period
✅ Get the chimney swept — it's a selling feature in fall
