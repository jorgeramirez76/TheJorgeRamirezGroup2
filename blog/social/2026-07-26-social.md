## POST 2: Cleaning Mistakes That Damage NJ Home Surfaces Before You List

**Article:** Cleaning Mistakes That Damage Your NJ Home Before You Even List It
**URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/cleaning-mistakes-damage-surfaces-nj-sellers-2026

---

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

I've walked through NJ homes where the seller clearly worked hard to clean before listing — and the cleaning itself created new problems.

Etched marble from a vinegar spray. Dull hardwood floors from a steam mop. Pitting on stainless appliances from bleach. Crumbling grout lines from years of chlorine bleach cleaning.

Every one of these shows up in listing photos. Every one gets documented by buyer inspectors. Every one becomes a credit demand.

The fix isn't cleaning less — it's using the right product for each surface. Marble and stone need pH-neutral cleaner. Hardwood needs Bona or equivalent, never wet. Stainless needs dedicated stainless cleaner, never bleach. Grout needs oxygen bleach paste, not chlorine.

Get this right before the deep clean and you don't create new problems in the process of fixing old ones.

### Instagram Caption

Cleaning your NJ home before listing it? Use the wrong product and you'll damage the surfaces buyers examine most.

Here's the quick version:

🚫 MARBLE & NATURAL STONE: No vinegar, no citrus cleaners, no Windex. Acid etches marble permanently — creates dull patches that require professional repair.
✅ Use: pH-neutral stone cleaner or warm water.

🚫 STAINLESS STEEL APPLIANCES: No bleach, no abrasive pads, no steel wool. Bleach corrodes stainless and causes permanent pitting.
✅ Use: Dedicated stainless cleaner, wipe with the grain.

🚫 HARDWOOD FLOORS: No wet mop, no steam mop, no vinegar, no ammonia cleaners. All of these degrade the finish and can cause warping.
✅ Use: Bona Hardwood Floor Cleaner + dry microfiber mop.

🚫 TILE GROUT: No chlorine bleach repeatedly. Over time it breaks down grout structure, allowing moisture penetration and mold.
✅ Use: Oxygen bleach paste (baking soda + 3% hydrogen peroxide, let sit 15 min, scrub, rinse).

One more thing: never mix bleach and ammonia. They produce toxic gas. Read every label before grabbing two products.

Get the right cleaner for each surface and your deep clean makes the home look better — not damaged.
