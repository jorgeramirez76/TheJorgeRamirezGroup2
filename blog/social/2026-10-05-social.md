## Article 2: The Glass Cooktop Mistake That Cracks Stovetops Before a NJ Home Sale

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/glass-cooktop-hot-lid-mistake-nj-home-sellers-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/05-kitchen.webp
**Instagram image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/05-kitchen.webp

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook sent a safety tip this weekend that most people have never heard: never set a hot pot lid face-down on a glass cooktop. The air under the lid cools and contracts, the lid seals itself to the glass like a suction cup, and prying it off is how cooktops chip and crack.

I bring it up because a cracked cooktop is the first thing a buyer sees on the range in a Summit or Millburn kitchen. It is not a cleaning item. It is a replacement, and if the range stays with the house, a known crack belongs on the New Jersey seller's disclosure.

A trivet beside the stove is the cheapest insurance in the kitchen. Lift pans instead of sliding them, clean sugar spills while they are warm, and stop using the cooktop as a counter while the house is listed.
