## Article 2: Is Your Furniture Hurting Your NJ Home Sale? The Visual Weight Problem
**URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/furniture-visual-weight-nj-home-staging

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)
Quick question for NJ sellers: when you take a wide-angle photo of your living room from the doorway, does the furniture look like it belongs in the space — or does it look like it's floating?

That's the visual weight problem. Furniture with thin, spindly legs reads as unstable and incomplete on a camera lens. In NJ's online-first market where buyers decide whether to schedule a showing based on listing photos, "floating furniture" is costing sellers showings.

The fix is often simple: an area rug to anchor the seating arrangement, swapping a glass coffee table for a wood one, centering the bed with nightstands on both sides.

## Article 3: Design Trends NJ Homeowners Regret Most Before Selling (2026)
**URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/design-trends-homeowners-regret-nj-2026

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)
If you renovated your NJ home between 2018 and 2022 — or you're planning to renovate before selling — this one's for you.

Some of the most popular design trends from that era are now working against sellers in Union, Essex, and Morris County. Grey everything, high-gloss all-white kitchens, shiplap in homes with no farmhouse architecture, and over-specialized rooms (the cigar lounge, the dedicated wine cellar) are the top four I see buyers pushing back on.

This isn't about the renovations being "bad." It's that trendy renovations narrow your buyer pool. Fewer buyers means less competition. Less competition means less leverage at offer time.
