### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

The first cool nights in Westfield and Morristown bring the first fogged windows. A little fog on the bathroom glass after a shower is normal. Water beading on every bedroom window by morning means the house is holding more moisture than it can shed.

House Outlook flagged window condensation this week, and the part sellers need to hear is what it leaves behind: stained sills, peeling paint, black spots in the sash corners. Inspectors do not write up fog. They write up the damage.

The fix is cheaper than new windows. Run the bath fans longer, turn down the furnace humidifier, put a dehumidifier in the basement, open the blinds during the day. Fix the air in September and the sills are dry and painted by the time the house is listed.

## Article 2: Where to Spend and Where to Save in the Living Room Before Selling an NJ Home

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/living-room-spend-vs-save-nj-home-sellers-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/04-brick-stone.webp
**Instagram image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/04-brick-stone.webp

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Ask a seller in Westfield or Summit where they spent money before listing and the living room is usually the answer, and usually the regret. New sofa, new rug, new coffee table, and the buyers still walked through in ninety seconds and asked about the roof.

Houzz ran a piece this week on where designers spend and save in a living room. Designers decorate for the people who live there. Sellers prepare the room for the people who live there next, and that changes the math.

Spend on paint, including trim and ceiling. Spend on lighting and on repairs a buyer will see. Save on the sofa, the art, the drapery and any fireplace remodel. Before you buy anything, carry half the furniture out and look again. Subtraction is free.
