## POST 2: The 20-Minute Evening Reset That Keeps Your NJ Home Show-Ready Every Day

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

One of the hardest parts of having your NJ home on the market isn't the listing or the photos — it's maintaining that level of clean every single day while your family is still living there.

Here's how the sellers who do it successfully approach it: they treat it like a job closing shift. Every night, 20 minutes. Kitchen first (8 minutes), bathrooms (4 minutes), living spaces (4 minutes), bedrooms (3 minutes), entry last (1 minute).

That structure is the difference between a house that's always ready for a 9am showing and a house that needs 90 minutes of scrambling every time the agent calls.

In the NJ market, buyers can legally request same-day showings. This routine is your answer to that.

🛋️ Living spaces — 4 minutes
→ Pillows fluffed, throws folded, remotes out of sight, lamps on low
→ Any mail, homework, shoes — gone

🛏️ Bedrooms — 3 minutes total
→ Beds made (pull-up counts), nightstand clear, laundry off the floor
→ Closet doors closed

🚪 Entry — 1 minute
→ Shoes in closet or bin, coats hung, nothing on the floor
→ Light on, mat clean

20 minutes. Done. Wake up to a home that's ready to show.

## POST 3: 9 Things NJ Home Sellers Should Clean Every Single Day While Listed

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

When your NJ home is actively listed, there's no such thing as "it can wait until the weekend."

Buyers are making snap judgments in the first 60 seconds of a showing. That means a sticky kitchen counter, a smudged bathroom mirror, or a dusty entryway aren't small things — they're the story the buyer tells themselves about your home.

Nine things to clean every single day while you're listed:
✓ Kitchen counters and sink
✓ Bathroom mirrors and faucets
✓ Entry and foyer floor
✓ All visible floors
✓ Light switches and door handles
✓ Pet bowls, beds, and any visible pet items
✓ Kitchen trash can exterior
✓ Stove exterior and knobs
✓ All glass surfaces (windows, cabinet fronts, oven door)

All nine together take under 25 minutes. That time is worth real money.
