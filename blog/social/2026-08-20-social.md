## POST 2: Homeowners Insurance & NJ Real Estate

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Something that's changing real estate deals in NJ right now: homeowners insurance costs.

Premiums are up 20-35% since 2023 across New Jersey — and lenders fold that number directly into your debt-to-income ratio. That means a $150/month insurance increase can knock $25,000+ off the loan you qualify for.

For buyers: get an insurance quote BEFORE finalizing your offer on a NJ home. Not after. Old roofs, oil tanks, outdated wiring — these drive premiums up and affect your buying power.

For sellers: homes with aging roofs, buried oil tanks, or older systems are creating friction in deals. Knowing your home's insurance profile before listing saves you painful renegotiations later.
