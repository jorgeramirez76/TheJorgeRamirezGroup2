## POST 1: The Smartest Place to Start Decluttering Your NJ Home Before You List

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Most NJ sellers start decluttering in the wrong place.

They hit the kitchen junk drawer. The bedroom closet. The bathroom vanity. And three weekends later, the same boxes are just in a different room.

Here's what my full-time NJ real estate work since 2017 has taught me: start with your dumping ground — the garage, the basement, or wherever everything ends up when there's nowhere else for it to go.

Clear that space first. Now you have a staging area. Now the rest of the house can actually get processed instead of just shifted around.

The garage also matters to NJ buyers directly. Above $700K, buyers are asking about two-car garage capacity before they ask about bedroom count. If yours is packed to the ceiling, that question answers itself — in the wrong direction.

## POST 2: 15 Things to Clear From Your NJ Kitchen Before You List Your Home

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

The kitchen makes or breaks NJ home sales. Not the primary bedroom. Not the living room. The kitchen.

And the difference between a kitchen that generates multiple offers and one that makes buyers hesitate is often not the countertops or the appliances — it's the clutter.

Here are the items I tell every NJ seller to remove from their kitchen before listing photos:
❌ Everything off the counters (yes, everything)
❌ The countertop appliance collection
❌ All the expired pantry items and faded spice jars
❌ Everything on the refrigerator door and the top of the fridge
❌ The under-sink cabinet chaos that inspectors and buyers both open
❌ Worn dish towels, chipped dishes, mismatched containers

A kitchen with clear counters photographs 30–40% larger. That's not an opinion — it's what I've watched happen across hundreds of NJ listings.
