## Post 3: Most NJ Homeowners Set Up Their Homes Backward

**Blog URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/homeowners-set-up-homes-backward-nj-2026

---

### Facebook Post (100–150 words)

Most NJ homeowners do it in this order:
Buy throw pillows → shop for furniture → pick a paint color → figure out the layout → wonder why nothing quite works.

Then they call me to sell the house — and I walk in and see the accumulated result of five or ten years of backward decisions.

The right order is almost the reverse. Function first. Layout second. Furniture to fit the layout. Paint to complement the furniture and the light. Lighting layered on top. Accessories last.

Get this right and your home is easier to live in, easier to stage, and easier to sell — for more money. Get it backward and you're fighting every single step of the listing process.
