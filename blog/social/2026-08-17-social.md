## Post 3: Hidden Costs First-Time NJ Homebuyers Don't Budget For

**Blog URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/hidden-costs-first-time-nj-buyers-2026

---

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

First-time NJ homebuyers — this one's for you. The purchase price is just the beginning. New Jersey has the highest property taxes in the country, mandatory attorney representation at closing, a 1% Mansion Tax on homes over $1M, and flood zone insurance requirements that catch buyers completely off guard.

Beyond your down payment, budget for: closing costs (2–3.5% of purchase price), NJ attorney fees ($1,200–$2,500), home inspection, first-year property taxes, homeowner's insurance, flood insurance if applicable, moving costs, and a 1% annual maintenance reserve. On a $750K home, that's often $32,000–$44,000 in first-year costs beyond the down payment.
