## POST 1: How Most NJ Homeowners Set Up Their Rooms Backward

### Facebook (100-150 words)
Most NJ homeowners decorate in the wrong order — and they never figure out why the room never looks right even after spending real money on it.

Interior designers say the correct sequence is: plan the room's function and traffic flow FIRST, then set your paint, flooring, and area rug, then bring in furniture, then decor last. Most people start with throw pillows and work backward. That's exactly why it feels off.

This matters most if you're thinking about selling. A room set up in the wrong sequence confuses buyers without them knowing why — and confused buyers write lower offers or walk away.
