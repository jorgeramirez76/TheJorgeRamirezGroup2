### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook’s newsletter this week linked a piece from The Home Edit on organizing habits that improve your mental health. The idea: clutter is a signal, and small repeated habits lower the noise.

Nothing tests those habits like living in a house that is for sale. A seller in Summit or Edison gets a showing request at 4 p.m. for 5:30 and has to make the house look like the photos. Willpower does not get you through six weeks of that. A system does.

The habits that matter while listed: a landing zone at the door you actually use, one object per flat surface, mail handled once, a ten-minute evening reset, a showing basket per floor, and a donation box in the garage that leaves weekly. Set it up in week one and the second month is easier than the first. Full guide: thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/organizing-habits-show-ready-nj-home-sellers-2026

## Article 3: Keep a Record of Your Home’s Paint Colors: The Small Habit That Pays Off When You Sell in NJ

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/home-paint-color-record-nj-home-sellers-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/03-shingle-porch.webp (crop to 1200×630)
**Instagram image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/properties/03-shingle-porch.webp (crop to 1080×1080)

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook published a simple trick this week: write each room’s paint brand, color, code and sheen on the back of a switch plate, and screw it back on. The record stays with the room.

Sellers need that record twice. First when the nail holes get patched before the photographer comes. Without the color and sheen, you are at the paint counter guessing between four whites, and the patch flashes in the photos. Second at the final walkthrough, when the buyer in Chatham or Millburn stands in the living room and asks what color this is.

Keep one labeled quart per color, not a basement of rusted gallons, which buyers read as deferred maintenance. Copy the list onto one page for the buyer. It is not required by NJ disclosure. It is goodwill, and goodwill has value in a deal. Full guide: thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/home-paint-color-record-nj-home-sellers-2026
