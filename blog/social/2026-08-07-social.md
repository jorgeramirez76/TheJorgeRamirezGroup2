## Post 2: Home Inspection Red Flags That Kill NJ Real Estate Deals in 2026

### Facebook Post
The most common reason NJ home deals fall apart after going under contract? Not financing. Not the appraisal. The home inspection.

After 15+ years and 500+ homes sold across NJ, I've seen the same issues blow up deals over and over: basement water intrusion, aging Federal Pacific electrical panels, HVAC systems without service records, roof age flags, and underground oil tanks on pre-1980 homes.

The fix is simple: know what's in your home BEFORE you list it. A pre-listing inspection ($400–$600) tells you exactly what a buyer's inspector will find. You then deal with issues on your terms — not under contract pressure with a closing date looming.
