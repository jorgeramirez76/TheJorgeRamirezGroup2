## Article 2: You’re Cleaning the Toilet Wrong: The Bathroom Detail NJ Buyers Notice at a Showing

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/toilet-seat-hinges-bathroom-detail-nj-home-showings-2026

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook ran a piece with a blunt headline: you are cleaning your toilet wrong. Everyone scrubs the bowl. Almost no one takes the seat off and cleans the hinges, which is where the grime actually lives.

I bring this up with sellers for a reason that has nothing to do with hygiene. A bathroom is the smallest room buyers walk into and the one where they stand closest to every surface. In a one-bath cape in Cranford, Union or Clark, that room is doing the work of three. A grimy hinge, a yellowed seat and a stained caulk line add up to the word buyers use in the car afterward: tired.

The five-minute pass: seat off, hinges and bolt area cleaned, base and floor cleaned, a fresh seat if the old one is worn, then flush, run the fan and press the floor. Full guide: thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/toilet-seat-hinges-bathroom-detail-nj-home-showings-2026

## Article 3: The Move-Out Question: The One Decluttering Test That Works When You’re Actually Selling a NJ Home

**Live URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/move-out-decluttering-question-nj-home-sellers-2026
**Image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/downsizing-nj-decision-guide-2026-1280.webp (crop to 1200×630)
**Instagram image:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/images/downsizing-nj-decision-guide-2026-1280.webp (crop to 1080×1080)

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

House Outlook published a decluttering approach it calls the Move Out Method. One question for every item: if I were moving, would I pay someone to pack and carry this? Most people have to imagine that. If you are listing a New Jersey home this fall, it is the actual bill.

Every item that stays in a Westfield colonial through closing gets packed, weighed and carried into whatever comes next, and the mover charges by the hour. It also costs you in the showing. A closet half full reads as storage. A closet packed to the door reads as too small, and buyers in Cranford and Maplewood capes already worry about closets.

Start with closets, garage and basement. Yes, no or not sure. Box the not-sure pile and date it. Pre-pack the yes items you will not touch before closing. Full guide: thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/move-out-decluttering-question-nj-home-sellers-2026
