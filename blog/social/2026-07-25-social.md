### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Every NJ seller thinks their home will sail through inspection. Then the report comes back with expired smoke detectors, a missing CO alarm outside the master bedroom, and a generator that's been stored in the garage for eight years.

Life-safety issues don't get negotiated the same way a dated kitchen does. Buyers and their attorneys treat them as material defects. In NJ, you also can't close without a Certificate of Smoke Detector and Carbon Monoxide Alarm Compliance — the CSCD — from your local fire department.

The fix for almost all of these issues costs under $50 and takes an afternoon. The cost of skipping it is a post-inspection negotiation where the buyer has documented leverage.

Every one of these issues is cheap and fast to fix. Every one of them shows up in inspection reports when they're wrong — and buyers use them as negotiating leverage.

### Instagram Caption

The cheapest thing you can do before listing your NJ home: clean the grout.

Not with expensive commercial cleaners. With a $2 bottle of hydrogen peroxide and a $1 box of baking soda.

Mix 2 parts baking soda to 1 part 3% hydrogen peroxide into a paste.
Apply to grout lines.
Wait 15 minutes.
Scrub with an old toothbrush.
Rinse.

Repeat if needed. On heavy staining, two rounds removes almost everything.

This combination breaks down the organic stains in grout — mold, mildew, soap scum — better than most commercial tile cleaners. And it works on tub floors, shower pans, and backsplash grout in the kitchen too.

One thing: never mix hydrogen peroxide with vinegar. Safe to use alone or with baking soda. Not safe combined with vinegar or bleach.

NJ buyers walking into a bathroom with clean grout and empty counters assume the home has been well maintained. NJ buyers walking into dark grout and product-covered counters assume the opposite — and it follows them through the rest of the showing.

Under $5. One afternoon. Real impact on your sale.
