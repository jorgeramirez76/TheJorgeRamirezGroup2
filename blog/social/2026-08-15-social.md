## Post 2: 12 Design Mistakes That Kill NJ Home Showings
**URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/design-mistakes-buyer-turnoffs-nj-2026

### Facebook Post
After 15 years of walking buyers through homes across Union, Morris, Essex, Hudson, and Middlesex counties, I can read a room within 60 seconds.

The homes that feel expensive are not always the ones with the best fixtures. They're the ones where the seller paid attention to scale, light, and layering. The homes that feel cheap are the ones where one or two common design mistakes undercut everything else.

And buyers can't always tell you what felt wrong. They just move on to the next listing.

I put together a detailed breakdown of the 12 design mistakes I see most often in NJ home showings — and exactly what to do about each one:

❌ Cool-temperature lighting that makes rooms feel clinical
❌ Short curtains that drag down ceiling height
❌ Rugs too small for the room (a 5x7 in a full living room is not a rug, it's a welcome mat)
❌ Small artwork on large walls
❌ No texture — everything flat and smooth
❌ Neglecting the entry where buyers form their first impression

Most of these fixes cost under $150. All of them matter more than you think.
