Quick guide for NJ sellers:

🌡️ WHITE COTTON TOWELS → Hot water (140°F)
Hot kills bacteria and restores brightness. Cold doesn't. This is the most common mistake.

🌡️ COLORED COTTON TOWELS → Warm water (104–120°F)
Hot water fades color over multiple washes. Warm cleans without damage.

🌡️ MICROFIBER TOWELS → Cold water (under 100°F)
Hot water breaks down microfiber structure. They'll feel stiff and scratchy after a few hot washes.

And every time: half a cup of white vinegar in the rinse cycle strips detergent buildup and eliminates musty odors. Skip the fabric softener — it clogs fibers and eventually traps smell.

Dry on the right heat setting immediately after washing. Never leave towels wet in the machine.

Your bathroom display towels should look and smell hotel-quality at every showing. This is how you get there.
