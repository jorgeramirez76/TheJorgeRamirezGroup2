## POST 2: 8 Things NJ Home Buyers Miss at Showings (That Cost Them Later)

---

### Facebook Post

Most NJ buyers walk a house and fall in love with the kitchen. That's fine. But here are the 8 things experienced buyers check at every showing — the ones that show up in the inspection report and drive post-contract negotiations.

1️⃣ HVAC age — furnace over 20 years = $8,000–$15,000 replacement conversation
2️⃣ Basement moisture — efflorescence and rust stains mean water history
3️⃣ Electrical panel brand — Federal Pacific and Zinsco are safety and insurance flags
4️⃣ Water pressure at multiple simultaneous fixtures
5️⃣ Foundation cracks — horizontal is structural, vertical is often cosmetic
6️⃣ Roof condition from the exterior — missing shingles, sagging ridgeline
7️⃣ Window seal failure — fogged panes mean the insulation value is gone
8️⃣ The neighborhood at different times of day — not just Sunday afternoon

The inspection will find all of this. But by then you're emotionally committed and already in contract. Walking in informed is always better.
