## POST 3: Why Most NJ Home Sellers Stage in the Wrong Order

**Blog URL:** https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/staging-order-nj-home-sellers-2026

---

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)

Here's the staging mistake I see in almost every NJ home I walk into before listing: sellers buy the furniture first, then try to figure out the room around it. That's the wrong order — and it costs money. The correct sequence is: floor plan and traffic flow first, then paint and floors, then lighting, then furniture, then accessories. Most sellers do it exactly backward. They buy throw pillows before they fix the paint. They arrange furniture before they understand traffic flow. They add lamps after listing photos are already taken. Every step done out of order makes the next step harder and more expensive. I give sellers a room-by-room pre-listing walk-through before any listing goes live. I'll tell you what order to address things and what to skip entirely.
