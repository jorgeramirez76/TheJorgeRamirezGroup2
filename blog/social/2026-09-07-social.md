## Post 1: How Dirty Baseboards Hurt Your NJ Home's First Impression
**Based on:** HouseOutlook — "How To Make Dust Slide Right Off Your Baseboards"

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)
Here's something that surprises a lot of NJ home sellers: buyers remember your baseboards.

Not consciously, necessarily — but dusty, grimy baseboards quietly signal "this home hasn't been cared for." That feeling affects offers.

The fix is easier than you think. A damp microfiber cloth with water and white vinegar knocks out the grime. Then wipe each baseboard with a dryer sheet — the anti-static residue repels dust and pet hair for 4–6 weeks. That's your whole listing period covered.

While you're at it, check the paint. Semi-gloss enamel on baseboards wipes clean and photographs better than flat paint. If they're yellowed or chipped, a fresh coat costs $20 and makes every room look sharper.

## Post 3: How Allergy-Proofing Your NJ Home Helps It Sell Faster
**Based on:** HouseOutlook — "The Ultimate Guide to Allergy-Proofing Your Home"

### Facebook Post (100-150 words)
New Jersey ranks among the worst states for allergy sufferers. And if your home triggers symptoms when buyers walk through it — sneezing, stuffiness, pet odors, stale air — you've lost them emotionally before they've even looked at the kitchen.

Clean air is a selling feature most NJ sellers ignore completely.

The steps that make the biggest difference: a HEPA-filter vacuum used twice a week, upgrading your HVAC filter to MERV 11 or higher, running air purifiers before showings (then moving them out of sight), managing pets out of the home during showings, and addressing any musty smell in the basement with a dehumidifier.

Buyers who feel comfortable in a home write strong offers. Buyers who feel unsettled look for problems. Clean air is part of confidence.
