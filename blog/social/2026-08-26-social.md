## Draft Set 1: Practical Pre-Listing Preparation

### Facebook

Thinking about pre-listing work? Start with condition and presentation, not a promised return.

A University of Texas at Arlington study of Dallas–Fort Worth sales found an association between overall curb appeal and sale price, with results varying by market conditions. That research is useful context, but it is not a forecast for a specific New Jersey home or project.

A practical first pass is to clean the entry, address visible maintenance, touch up paint where appropriate, and get quotes before replacing anything. For older homes, ask about lead-safe practices. Use qualified professionals for electrical or other regulated work.

Before spending, compare the property’s condition, likely buyer questions, and current local comparable sales.

## Draft Set 2: Staging Without Trend Chasing

### Facebook

Good staging is less about chasing a design trend and more about helping people understand a home’s space and function.

In the National Association of REALTORS® 2025 national survey, 83% of buyers’ agents said staging made it easier for a buyer to visualize a property as a future home. That is reported national survey context—not a guarantee of a higher offer, a faster sale, or a specific New Jersey outcome.

A useful first pass is to reduce clutter, make each room’s purpose clear, improve lighting where needed, and keep listing photos accurate. Recommendations should reflect the home’s condition, price range, and current local competition.
