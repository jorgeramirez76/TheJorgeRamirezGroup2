### Facebook

Most sellers in New Jersey don't need a renovation before listing. They need a plan.

Thirty days is enough time to make a real difference in how your home shows to buyers—if you use those days in the right order. Declutter first (this is the most impactful step), then tackle repairs and touch-ups, then focus on presentation and staging, then lock in your listing details: professional photography, pricing review, and disclosure prep.

The sellers I work with who go through this process consistently tell me the same thing: they had no idea how much better the house looked once they cleared it out. Buyers can't see past clutter. They can't see past deferred maintenance signals. Your job in the month before listing is to remove those mental obstacles.

You don't need a renovation to get your NJ home ready for buyers. You need 30 days and a plan. 📋

Here's the breakdown:

📦 **Week 1 — Declutter & Deep Clean**
Rent storage, clear every surface, deep clean everything. Buyers can't see past clutter.

🔧 **Week 2 — Repairs & Touch-Ups**
Address what inspectors will find: dripping faucets, inoperative windows, missing GFCI outlets, peeling exterior paint.

🛋️ **Week 3 — Staging & Presentation**
Clear countertops. Maximize light. Make every room read clearly as what it is.

📸 **Week 4 — Listing Prep**
Professional photos. Pricing review based on current comparables. Disclosure documentation.

The sellers who come to market prepared get better results than those who don't. That's true in any NJ market, at any price point.
