## POST 2: What NJ Home Buyers Are Willing to Pay More For in Fall 2026
URL: https://thejorgeramirezgroup.com/blog/what-nj-buyers-pay-more-for-fall-2026

### Facebook (100–150 words)

The NJ housing market in fall 2026 is not balanced — and good homes in good condition still go over ask with multiple offers. But the gap between a fast, clean, over-ask sale and a 45-day market situation is wider than it's been in years.

After 15 years and 500+ closings across Union, Essex, Morris, and Middlesex counties, I can tell you exactly what's driving those premium offers right now:

✅ A real, dedicated home office (buyers are paying $15K–$40K more)
✅ Move-in-ready condition — no punch list
✅ An updated kitchen, even a moderate one
✅ Energy efficiency with documentation
✅ A properly permitted outdoor space

And I'll tell you what buyers are using to negotiate DOWN — roof age, oil heat, original HVAC, and unfinished basements.
