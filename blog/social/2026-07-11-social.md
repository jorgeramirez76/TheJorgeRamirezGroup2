### Instagram Caption

Your NJ home is always dusty — and there's a reason no one tells you about. 🏠

NJ's older housing stock (most Colonials + Capes built 1950-1990) runs forced-air HVAC systems with aging ductwork. When the filter is clogged or the ducts are dirty, the system redistributes dust through every room every single time it cycles.

You clean at 8am. By 2pm, the surfaces are coated again.

And buyers notice when they walk in. It registers as "the home feels tired" — even when they can't articulate why. It shows in listing photos. It shows in inspection reports. And it shows in offers.

The fix is NOT complicated:
🔹 Replace HVAC filter (MERV-8 minimum) — first
🔹 Run system 24 hours to capture airborne dust
🔹 Clean/replace supply registers
🔹 Consider duct cleaning if system is 10+ years old
🔹 Get HVAC serviced — get a report you can share with buyers
🔹 THEN do the professional deep clean

Sequence matters. Most sellers do it backward.
