A repeatable research method

  1. Name the scope first: county, legal municipality or tax district, or individual property.
  2. Record the source, exact field label, reporting year or period, geography, and access date.
  3. Compare only compatible geographies, periods, property categories, and field definitions.
  4. For one home, replace broad context with current comparable properties and verified subject-property facts.

These public averages are historical district context. They are not a listing price, appraisal, forecast, or prediction of a transaction result.
