Start with the current county report

New Jersey Realtors provides a public path to state and county reports. Use the source controls so the county, reporting period, and available property category are explicit.

  1. Open the New Jersey Realtors market-data page.
  2. Follow its link to the direct public county-report portal.
  3. Select Union County, then the exact period and available property category.
  4. Keep the source's metric labels and period together in your notes; do not combine unlike periods.

For a 2026 review, record the exact month, year-to-date range, or other period displayed by the source.

Three different questions require three different scopes

County

A Union County result is a county-level reference. A county result does not describe every municipality.

Municipality

Use official county or municipal records for the legal municipality. County labels, municipality names, mailing names, and ZIP labels are not interchangeable.

Property

A property-specific CMA needs current comparable properties plus the subject property's condition, features, location, and timing. County context is not a property valuation.

A defensible comparison workflow

  1. Name the geography first: county, legal municipality, or individual property.
  2. Record the source, release, selected period, property category, and access date.
  3. Compare only identical metric labels and compatible periods; keep averages distinct from medians.
  4. Return to the original source before making a decision because public reports can be revised or replaced.

This page is a source map and research method. It does not publish a copied market table, municipality ranking, or property valuation.
