01 · Read the service pattern

“Midtown Direct” describes individual service, not a blanket station promise

The official Morris & Essex Lines timetable effective May 31, 2026 lists station calls by train column and identifies New York Penn service with an MD marker. The endpoint can differ across trains, directions, days, and operating conditions.

A station on the corridor does not mean every train is direct to New York Penn Station. Verify the individual train, terminal, transfer pattern, travel date, and return in NJ TRANSIT’s current trip planner.

02 · Station names and municipalities

Selected station areas, shown without ranking them

These examples are illustrative, not exhaustive. A station name, postal label, and legal municipality can differ. Confirm the municipality for a specific parcel before using tax, school, zoning, permit, or public-record information.

South Orange

South Orange Village · Essex County

The station name aligns with South Orange Village. The Village’s current commuter-parking page distinguishes this facility from Mountain Station.

Mountain Station

South Orange Village · Essex County

Mountain Station is a station name within South Orange Village, not a separate municipality. Compare its access and facility details separately from South Orange station.

Maplewood

Maplewood Township · Essex County

The station name corresponds to Maplewood Township in the official state municipality lookup. Check the current train and local station-access plan for the property.

Millburn

Millburn Township · Essex County

Millburn station is associated with Millburn Township. Do not treat the nearby Short Hills station label as a separate municipality.

Short Hills

Millburn Township · Essex County

Short Hills is an unincorporated community within Millburn Township. Property records and municipal services should be checked under Millburn Township.

Summit

City of Summit · Union County

The timetable shows Summit in both Morristown Line and Gladstone Branch train patterns. Check the branch, terminal, and individual itinerary instead of assuming one pattern.

New Providence

Borough of New Providence · Union County

New Providence and Murray Hill stations are both served by commuter parking lots managed by the Borough of New Providence.

Murray Hill

Borough of New Providence · Union County

Murray Hill is a station-area label, while the Borough’s official service page places the commuter facility under New Providence administration.

Chatham

Chatham Borough · Morris County

Chatham station is in Chatham Borough; Chatham Township is a separate municipality. Confirm the parcel municipality rather than relying on “Chatham” alone.

Madison

Borough of Madison · Morris County

The station name corresponds to Madison Borough in the official municipality lookup. Use NJ TRANSIT’s live tools for the specific train and terminal.

Convent Station

Morris Township · Morris County

Convent Station is a station-area and mailing name in Morris Township. Morris Township’s official page illustrates why the mailing label should not replace a parcel-level municipality check.

Morristown

Town of Morristown · Morris County

Morristown and Morris Township are separate municipalities. The station name maps to Morristown Town in the official state municipality lookup.

03 · Property-specific test

Compare the whole trip in both directions

A station-area label is only the starting point. Run the real schedule and the real access plan before deciding whether a property works for your routine.

Exact service window

Use the required arrival date and time, then repeat the check for the return. Confirm the terminal and any connection for each itinerary.

Property to platform

Inspect the walking route, drive, drop-off, bicycle plan, facility entrance, parking operator, and accessibility needs.

Terminal to destination

Add the subway, PATH, bus, ferry, walking, or other final leg that applies to the actual destination.

Current conditions

Review alerts, construction, facility notices, parking rules, and alternative routes before travel and again before a housing decision.

05 · Common questions

Midtown Direct planning questions

What does the MD marker mean in the current NJ TRANSIT timetable?

The Morris & Essex Lines timetable uses an MD marker on individual train columns to identify service to or from New York Penn Station. Check the marker, terminal, date, and direction for the train you plan to use.

Does every Morris & Essex or Gladstone Branch train serve New York Penn Station directly?

No. A station on the corridor does not mean every train is direct to New York Penn Station. Terminals and connections vary by individual train and service condition, so verify both directions in NJ TRANSIT’s live trip planner.

Are Short Hills, Chatham, and Convent Station municipality names?

Not in every case. Short Hills is within Millburn Township, the Chatham rail station is in Chatham Borough while Chatham Township is separate, and Convent Station is a station-area and mailing name within Morris Township.

How should I compare a property-to-office commute?

Run the exact travel date and arrival window in the official planner, check the return, and add the property-to-platform and terminal-to-destination legs. Recheck alerts, parking rules, and accessibility information.

Why does this guide omit fixed travel times, fares, and housing figures?

Those details can vary by train, date, operator, property, and market period. This guide keeps the planning framework stable and sends readers to the responsible current source for changing facts.
