Stay NJ 2026: What New Jersey Seniors Get and How to Apply

Last updated  ·  August 2026

New Jersey’s Stay NJ program pays eligible homeowners aged 65 and older toward their property taxes. Here is who qualifies, how much it pays, and the one deadline that matters.

See the deadline Talk through your options

Stay NJ pays eligible New Jersey homeowners aged 65 and older up to $6,500 toward their property taxes, with the maximum set by income. You claim it on Form PAS-1, the same single application that covers Senior Freeze and ANCHOR, and the deadline to apply for the 2025 PAS-1 is November 2, 2026.

Every figure on this page comes from the NJ Division of Taxation’s Stay NJ page. Programs change; confirm the current numbers with the Division or your tax preparer before you rely on them.

Who qualifies for Stay NJ?

The Division lists four conditions. You (or your spouse) must have:

  • Been age 65 or older during 2025
  • Owned and lived in a home for the full 12 months of 2025
  • Resided in a home subject to property taxes or a P.I.L.O.T. (payment-in-lieu-of-tax) agreement
  • Income of $200,000 or less

Renters and mobile home owners are not eligible for Stay NJ. That is a hard line, and it is the single most common reason a hopeful applicant is turned away. If you rent, look at ANCHOR instead, which does cover renters.

The full-12-months rule catches people out. If you bought or sold mid-2025, you did not own and occupy that home for all of 2025, and that year does not qualify — even if you are 70 and have owned NJ property for decades.

How much does Stay NJ pay?

The maximum is tiered by income:

IncomeMaximum benefit
$0 – $100,000$6,500
$100,000.01 – $150,000$5,000
$150,000.01 – $200,000$4,000
Over $200,000$0

These are maximums, not guaranteed amounts. The Division states the figures are presented on an annualized basis assuming no changes to the Stay NJ program in the Fiscal Year 2028 budget — in other words, they depend on what the State budgets. Treat the table as the ceiling, not the check.

One form covers all three programs

This is the part most people get wrong. You do not file three applications. Applicants file Form PAS-1 to claim benefits from Senior Freeze, ANCHOR and Stay NJ together. The application can be downloaded or submitted online, and you receive a benefit statement showing your Senior Freeze, ANCHOR and Stay NJ amounts.

The deadline: November 2, 2026

The deadline to apply for the 2025 PAS-1 is November 2, 2026. One form, three programs, one date.

If you are reading this in late summer, you have weeks, not months. The application asks for income and residency details for the 2025 calendar year, so pull your 2025 records before you sit down with it.

What this means if you are deciding whether to downsize

This is where I can actually help, and where I want to be straight with you: Stay NJ changes the math, not the decision.

If property taxes are the main reason you are thinking about leaving your home, a credit of a few thousand dollars a year is a real offset and worth running before you list. Compare it against what you would actually net from a sale and what a smaller place in the same town would cost you — including its taxes, which are often not as much lower as people assume.

But if the reason is the stairs, the maintenance, the fact that you are two hours from your kids, or that the house is simply bigger than your life is now, a tax credit does not touch any of that. I have sat with enough NJ homeowners on this exact question to say plainly: the money is usually not the deciding factor, and pretending it is tends to delay a move people are glad they made.

Common questions

Who qualifies for Stay NJ?

Age 65 or older during 2025, owned and lived in the home for all 12 months of 2025, home subject to property taxes or P.I.L.O.T., and income of $200,000 or less. Renters and mobile home owners are not eligible.

How much is the Stay NJ benefit?

Up to $6,500 if income is $100,000 or less; up to $5,000 from $100,000.01 to $150,000; up to $4,000 from $150,000.01 to $200,000; nothing above $200,000. The Division presents these as annualized maximums assuming no program changes in the FY2028 budget.

What is the Stay NJ deadline?

November 2, 2026 for the 2025 PAS-1.

Do I apply separately for ANCHOR and Senior Freeze?

No. Form PAS-1 claims all three where you qualify, and you get one benefit statement showing each amount.

Does Stay NJ change whether I should downsize?

It changes the carrying cost of staying. If taxes are the reason you are considering a move, run the credit into your numbers first. If the reason is stairs, upkeep, or distance from family, the credit does not address it.
