Millburn NJ Real Estate Market Report 2026

Last updated  ·  April 2026

Millburn, NJ is a charming township located in Essex County, offering a unique blend of suburban tranquility and urban convenience. As a homebuyer, you'll find that Millburn is divided into distinct neighborhoods, each with its own character and advantages. In this spotlight guide, we'll explore 5 of these areas, highlighting their vibe, price range, and suitability for different lifestyles. ### 1. Downtown Millburn Downtown Millburn is the heart of the township, bustling with activity. This area is perfect for those who crave a walkable, urban lifestyle. The vibe here is lively, with a mix of boutique shops, restaurants, and bars. Home prices in Downtown Millburn range from $400,000 to over $1 million for larger, more luxurious homes. This area is best for singles and couples who enjoy the convenience of having amenities at their doorstep. Families might find the area appealing as well, thanks to the excellent schools and community events, but the housing stock is more suited to smaller families or those looking for a more urban, low-maintenance lifestyle. ### 2. Millburn Township (South of Downtown) The broader Millburn Township area, south of Downtown, offers a more suburban feel. Homes here are generally larger, with bigger yards, making it an ideal location for families. The price range in this area is from $500,000 to $1.2 million, reflecting the larger size of the properties and the highly rated schools. This neighborhood is best for families due to its quieter, more residential vibe and the availability of spacious homes. However, it's also a great option for downsizers who want to maintain a suburban lifestyle without the need for extensive yard work, as many homes are on manageable sized lots. ### 3. Short Hills Short Hills is an upscale neighborhood known for its beautiful, mature trees and expansive homes. The vibe here is distinctly suburban, with a strong sense of community. Prices for homes in Short Hills can range from $700,000 to over $2 million, reflecting the high quality of life and the large, beautifully landscaped properties. This area is best for families and downsizers who prioritize privacy, space, and a serene environment. While it's an excellent choice for those seeking a tranquil lifestyle, singles might find the area less appealing due to its more laid-back, family-oriented atmosphere. ### 4. The Wyoming Section The Wyoming Section of Millburn is a charming, older neighborhood with a lot of character. It's known for its beautiful, established trees and the classic look of its homes. The price range here is a bit more variable, from $350,000 to $900,000, depending on the size, age, and condition of the home. This area has a quaint, small-town feel that's hard to find in other parts of Millburn. It's best for families and singles who appreciate historic homes and a strong sense of community. However, downsizers might also enjoy the area, especially those looking for a more affordable, yet still highly desirable, location. ### 5. The Springfield Avenue Area The Springfield Avenue area, while technically part of the broader township, has its own unique charm. It's a bit more commercial, with a mix of residential and business properties. Home prices here can range from $300,000 to $700,000, making it one of the more affordable options in Millburn. The vibe is more transitional, with a blend of older and newer constructions. This area is best for singles and couples who want easy access to amenities and public transportation. Families might also consider this area for its practicality and the fact that it offers a range of housing options, including more affordable choices. In conclusion, Millburn, NJ, offers a diverse range of lifestyles and home options, each suited to different needs and preferences. Whether you're a family, single, or downsizer, there's a neighborhood in Millburn that can provide the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and community.
